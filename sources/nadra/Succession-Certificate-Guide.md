# Succession Certificate — Pakistan

## 1. Overview

A **Succession Certificate** is a legal certificate issued to the legal heirs of a deceased Pakistani citizen for the transfer/collection of the deceased person's **movable assets**.

NADRA has been authorized under the applicable federal and provincial succession laws to issue:

- **Succession Certificates** — for movable assets.
- **Letters of Administration** — for immovable property/assets.

The NADRA process allows eligible legal heirs to obtain the certificate without going through the conventional court process, subject to the applicable law and verification requirements.

---

## 2. Eligibility

According to NADRA, the following conditions apply:

- The **deceased must be a Pakistani citizen**.
- The **legal heirs must be Pakistani citizens**.
- The relevant assets/property must be **located within Pakistan**.
- One of the legal heirs must apply in person at a designated NADRA site.
- In **Sindh and Balochistan**, applications may be made through legal counsel according to NADRA's stated procedure.

---

## 3. When a Succession Certificate Is Required

A Succession Certificate is generally used for **movable assets**, such as:

- Bank accounts
- Cash and deposits
- Shares/securities
- Other movable financial assets belonging to the deceased

For **immovable property**, such as land or a house, the relevant document is generally a **Letter of Administration** rather than a Succession Certificate.

---

## 4. Required Documents

NADRA currently lists the following requirements:

### 1. Death Certificate

The official death certificate of the deceased.

### 2. CNIC Cancellation Certificate

The deceased person's **CNIC Cancellation Certificate issued by NADRA**.

### 3. Family Registration Certificate (FRC)

The **FRC** is used by NADRA to help establish and validate the family relationship of the legal heirs.

### 4. List of Legal Heirs

A complete list of the legal heirs, together with copies of their:

- CNICs, or
- Other applicable identity documents.

### 5. Asset / Property Details

Details of the deceased person's:

- Movable assets
- Immovable property, where relevant to the application for a Letter of Administration

### 6. Letter of Authorization / Affidavit

A **Letter of Authorization/Affidavit** attested by an **Oath Commissioner** is required.

NADRA also provides an official affidavit/authorization form for Letter of Administration/Succession Certificate applications.

---

## 5. Step-by-Step Application Process

### Step 1 — Obtain the Death Certificate

Obtain the official death certificate of the deceased.

---

### Step 2 — Cancel the Deceased's CNIC

Apply to NADRA for cancellation of the deceased person's CNIC.

Obtain the **CNIC Cancellation Certificate**.

---

### Step 3 — Obtain the FRC

Obtain a **Family Registration Certificate (FRC)** showing the relevant family relationships.

The FRC helps NADRA validate the legal-heir information.

---

### Step 4 — Prepare the Legal-Heir Information

Prepare:

- Complete list of legal heirs
- Copies of their CNICs
- Details of the deceased
- Details of the assets
- Relevant family/relationship documents

---

### Step 5 — Prepare Authorization / Affidavit

Complete the required **Letter of Authorization/Affidavit** and have it attested by an **Oath Commissioner**.

---

### Step 6 — Visit NADRA

One of the legal heirs must appear in person at a designated NADRA site with the required documentation.

For applications in **Sindh and Balochistan**, NADRA states that applications may be processed through legal counsel.

---

### Step 7 — Submit the Application

NADRA verifies:

- Identity of the deceased
- Identity of the legal heirs
- Family relationships
- Asset information
- Supporting documents

The applicant is responsible for providing accurate information. NADRA warns that false information or forged documents may result in legal proceedings.

---

### Step 8 — Publication / Notice Process

After the application is processed, the required notice/publication procedure is carried out.

The NADRA page states that the processing period is **14 days after publication**.

---

### Step 9 — Issuance

After completion of the required verification and legal process, NADRA issues the:

- **Succession Certificate** for movable assets, or
- **Letter of Administration** for immovable property.

---

## 6. Fees

NADRA's current fee structure lists the following fees for issuance of a Succession Certificate and decline on a processed application:

| Service | Assets ≥ PKR 100,000 | Assets < PKR 100,000 |
|---|---:|---:|
| New / Fresh Application | PKR 20,000 | PKR 10,000 |
| Duplicate Application | PKR 5,000 | PKR 5,000 |
| Amended Application | PKR 5,000 | PKR 5,000 |
| Decline on Processed Application | PKR 15,000 | PKR 15,000 |
| Office Mistake | Free | Free |

### Sindh Additional Fee

NADRA's current fee table specifically lists:

> **PKR 22,000 in Sindh** for a new/fresh application where the asset value is **PKR 100,000 or more**.

Therefore:

| Location / Asset Value | Fresh Application Fee |
|---|---:|
| General — ≥ PKR 100,000 | PKR 20,000 |
| **Sindh — ≥ PKR 100,000** | **PKR 22,000** |
| Assets < PKR 100,000 | PKR 10,000 |

These figures should be checked against NADRA's fee structure at the time of application because government fees can change.

---

## 7. Processing Time

NADRA's Succession Certificate page states:

**Processing time: 14 days after publication.**

The actual overall time may therefore depend on:

- Completion of documentation
- Verification of legal heirs
- Publication/notice requirements
- Any objections or discrepancies
- Additional verification required by NADRA

---

## 8. Succession Certificate vs Letter of Administration

| Feature | Succession Certificate | Letter of Administration |
|---|---|---|
| Main purpose | Movable assets | Immovable property |
| Example | Bank account, shares | Land, house |
| Applicant | Legal heirs | Legal heirs |
| Issuing authority | NADRA under applicable succession laws | NADRA under applicable succession laws |
| Basic family verification | FRC / NADRA records | FRC / NADRA records |
| Death certificate | Required | Required |
| CNIC cancellation | Required | Required |

---

## 9. Important Points

- The deceased and legal heirs must meet NADRA's Pakistani-citizenship requirement.
- Assets/property must be located within Pakistan.
- One legal heir must normally appear in person at the designated NADRA site.
- Sindh and Balochistan have a specific provision for processing through legal counsel.
- A complete and accurate list of legal heirs is important.
- False information or forged documents can lead to legal proceedings.
- A **Succession Certificate** concerns movable assets, while a **Letter of Administration** concerns immovable property.
- NADRA states that the processing period is **14 days after publication**.
- Fees depend on the value of the assets and, in some cases, the province.
