# Cancellation Certificate — NADRA

## 1. Overview

A **Cancellation Certificate** is issued by the **National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA)** when a person's identity document or registration is cancelled or revoked.

Under Section 17(3) of the NADRA Ordinance, 2000, NADRA issues a Cancellation Certificate in cases where an identity document/registration is cancelled because of:

- Death
- Surrender of Pakistani nationality
- Resumption of Pakistani nationality
- Other applicable cases of ineligibility
- Divorce, where applicable to POC cancellation

NADRA currently provides Cancellation Certificate services through its identity-document system and PakID services.

---

## 2. Types of Cancellation

### A. Cancellation Due to Death

This is used when a Pakistani citizen has died and their CNIC, CRC, or NICOP needs to be cancelled.

#### Eligibility

- The person whose identity document is being cancelled must be deceased.
- A blood relative or other eligible applicant must provide proof of death.

#### Common Requirements

- Death Certificate issued by:
  - Union Council / relevant local authority in Pakistan, or
  - Appropriate authority in a foreign country.
- Identity document details of the deceased, such as:
  - CNIC
  - NICOP
  - CRC
- Applicant's own identity document.
- Relationship information, where required.

---

## 3. Cancellation Due to Surrender of Pakistani Nationality

This service is used when a person formally surrenders Pakistani nationality and their Pakistani identity documents need to be cancelled.

### Eligibility

A person who has formally surrendered Pakistani nationality and is required to have their Pakistani identity documents cancelled.

### Documents

Depending on the case, NADRA may require:

- Proof/documentation of nationality surrender.
- Pakistani CNIC/NICOP/CRC details.
- Foreign passport or nationality documentation, where applicable.
- Other supporting documents requested by NADRA.

---

## 4. Cancellation Due to Resumption of Nationality

NADRA's PakID services also list **Cancellation Certificate due to Resumption**.

This service applies to cases where an identity-document record requires cancellation/updated processing following the resumption of Pakistani nationality.

The applicant should provide the relevant nationality/resumption documentation and Pakistani identity-document information.

NADRA may request additional supporting documents depending on the individual case.

---

## 5. POC Cancellation

NADRA also provides cancellation services for **Pakistan Origin Cards (POC)**.

POC cancellation may be applicable because of:

- Death
- Divorce
- Surrender of nationality

The applicable reason and supporting documentation must be provided with the application.

---

## 6. Required Documents

The exact requirements depend on the reason for cancellation.

### For Death

- Death Certificate
- CNIC/NICOP/CRC information of deceased
- Applicant's identity document
- Proof of relationship, where required

### For Nationality Surrender

- Nationality surrender documentation
- CNIC/NICOP/CRC details
- Foreign nationality/passport documentation, where applicable
- Additional supporting documents if requested

### For POC Cancellation Due to Divorce

- POC information
- Divorce documentation
- Relevant spouse/family information
- Additional documents if requested by NADRA

> **Important:** NADRA may request additional documents or verification depending on the applicant's circumstances.

---

## 7. Application Through PakID

NADRA's official PakID system lists Cancellation Certificate services including:

- Due to Death
- Due to Surrender
- Due to Resumption
- Due to Divorce

### Step 1 — Open PakID

Use the official NADRA PakID mobile application.

### Step 2 — Sign In

Create an account or sign in to an existing PakID account.

### Step 3 — Select Cancellation Certificate

Select the appropriate cancellation service.

For example:

**Cancellation Certificate → Due to Death**

or another applicable category.

### Step 4 — Enter Information

Provide the requested information about:

- Applicant
- Person whose document is being cancelled
- Identity document
- Reason for cancellation

### Step 5 — Upload Documents

Upload the supporting documents required for the selected cancellation category.

### Step 6 — Verification

Complete any required verification or biometric process.

NADRA may require an applicant to visit a Registration Centre if additional verification is necessary.

### Step 7 — Pay the Applicable Fee

Pay the applicable fee according to the cancellation category.

### Step 8 — Submit Application

Review the information and submit the application.

### Step 9 — Track Application

Track the application through the PakID system.

---

## 8. Application Through NADRA Registration Centre

The general process is:

1. Visit a NADRA Registration Centre.
2. Request the relevant Cancellation Certificate service.
3. Provide the required identity information.
4. Submit supporting documents.
5. Complete verification/biometrics if required.
6. Pay the applicable fee.
7. Receive the application/receipt information.
8. Wait for processing.
9. Receive the Cancellation Certificate according to the applicable delivery procedure.

---

## 9. Fees and Processing Time

According to NADRA's current fee structure:

### CNIC / CRC Cancellation Due to Death

| Service | Fee | Processing Time |
|---|---:|---:|
| CNIC/CRC Cancellation due to Death | PKR 0 | 1 day |

---

### NICOP Cancellation Due to Death

| Service | Fee | Processing Time |
|---|---:|---:|
| NICOP Cancellation due to Death | USD 0 | 1 day |

This applies to the cancellation service listed by NADRA for overseas applicants.

---

### NICOP/CNIC/CRC Cancellation Due to Surrender of Nationality

NADRA's current fee structure lists:

| Service | Fee | Processing Time |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cancellation due to Surrender of Nationality | USD 15 | 2 days |

---

### POC Cancellation

| Reason | Fee | Processing Time |
|---|---:|---:|
| Due to Death | USD 25 | 2 days |
| Due to Divorce | USD 25 | 2 days |
| Due to Surrender of Nationality | USD 15 | 2 days |

These are the current fees and timelines published by NADRA and may be updated by the Authority.

---

## 10. Important Points

- A Cancellation Certificate is different from simply losing or replacing an identity card.
- Cancellation due to death requires proof of death.
- Cancellation because of nationality surrender requires the relevant nationality-surrender documentation.
- NADRA may request additional documents or verification.
- The fee depends on the type of identity document and reason for cancellation.
- Processing times shown by NADRA are priority/service timelines and may be affected if additional verification is required.
- For overseas applications, fees are generally listed in USD.
