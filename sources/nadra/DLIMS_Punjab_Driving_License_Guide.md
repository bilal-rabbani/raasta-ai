# DLIMS Punjab — Driving License Services Guide

**Official portal:** https://dlims.punjab.gov.pk (now integrated with the **Dastak** — One Window for all Government Services — platform)
**Contact:** Emergency: 15 | Phone: 042-99202892 | Email: info@dlims.punjab.gov.pk

---

## 1. Learner Driving License

**Purpose:** First step before applying for a regular license.

**Eligibility / Age limits:**
- Motorcycle / Motor Car: 18 years
- LTV (Rickshaw, Taxi, Tractor Commercial): 21 years
- Not issued to persons with certain disabilities restricting safe driving

**Required documents:**
- Recent passport-size photograph (plain white background)
- Scanned copy/picture of original CNIC — front
- Scanned copy/picture of original CNIC — back
- Medical Certificate signed by a government doctor (mandatory only if applicant is 50+ years old, or applying for a commercial category)

**Application process:**
1. Create account / log in at dlims.punjab.gov.pk (or via Dastak)
2. Fill out the learner's license application form
3. Upload the required documents
4. Generate PSID (Payment Slip ID) and pay via ATM, online banking, or mobile banking
5. Print/download the digital learner license from your account

**Fee:** Rs. 500 per category (e.g., bike, car) — per DLIMS official FAQ, effective under the Dastak-DLIMS integration (2026)

**Processing time:** Issued instantly after form submission and payment confirmation

**Validity:** Learner permits are typically valid for a limited period (commonly cited as 6 months) before a regular license must be obtained

## 2. Regular Driving License

**Process:**
1. Create account & log in
2. Fill application form
3. Generate PSID and pay
4. Payment confirmation
5. Administrative approval
6. Processing & dispatch (delivered by courier)

**Validity options:** 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 years (maximum), chosen by the applicant

**Official Fee Structure (per category, fees apply for one year of validity):**

| Category | Test Fee | Fee After Passing Test | Courier Fee | Total (Excl. Test Fee) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M/Cycle | Rs. 50 | Rs. 450 | Rs. 480 | Rs. 930 |
| Rickshaw | Rs. 100 | Rs. 400 | Rs. 480 | Rs. 880 |
| Car/Jeep | Rs. 150 | Rs. 1,350 | Rs. 480 | Rs. 1,830 |
| Tractor Agri | Rs. 50 | Rs. 950 | Rs. 480 | Rs. 1,430 |
| Tractor Commercial | Rs. 100 | Rs. 1,400 | Rs. 480 | Rs. 1,880 |
| LTV | Rs. 150 | Rs. 1,850 | Rs. 480 | Rs. 2,330 |
| Invalid Carriage | Rs. 0 | Rs. 0 | Rs. 480 | Rs. 480 |
| M/Cycle + Car/Jeep | Rs. 200 | Rs. 1,350 | Rs. 480 | Rs. 1,830 |
| M/Cycle + LTV | Rs. 200 | Rs. 1,850 | Rs. 480 | Rs. 2,330 |

*Note: If applying for multiple categories in combination, the fee of the highest-fee category applies.*

## 6. Application Tracking

- **Track Application:** https://dlims.punjab.gov.pk/track
- Applicants can track the status of: new license applications, renewals, duplicate licenses, international permits, and learner permit applications.
- Typically requires the applicant's CNIC number or the tracking/PSID reference generated at submission.

---

## 7. License Verification

- **Verify License:** https://dlims.punjab.gov.pk/verify
- Used to confirm the authenticity and current status (valid, expired, under process) of a driving license — useful for employers, traffic authorities, or the license holder themselves.
- Typically requires the license number or the holder's CNIC.

---

## 8. Required Documents (Summary — applies broadly across services)

| Document | Requirement |
|---|---|
| CNIC (front & back, scanned/photo) | Mandatory for all services |
| Passport-size photograph | Mandatory for all services |
| Medical Certificate (govt. doctor) | Mandatory if applicant is 50+ years, or for certain commercial/heavy categories |
| Existing driving license | Required for renewal, duplicate, and international license applications |
| Valid passport with visa | Required for International Driving License where applicable |

**File format guidance:** Documents must be clear, legible, within specified size limits, and uploaded as PDF or JPEG.

## Notes on Sources & Accuracy

- Fee tables for **Regular License, Renewal, Duplicate, and International License** are taken directly from the official DLIMS fee structure page (dlims.punjab.gov.pk/fee_structure).
- The **Learner License fee (Rs. 500/category)** reflects the current figure stated in DLIMS's own FAQ following the 2026 Dastak-DLIMS integration; older third-party sources cite a much lower historical figure (~Rs. 60 postal ticket), which appears outdated.
- Tracking and verification page descriptions are based on the general functionality described by DLIMS and secondary guides, since the live pages require interactive input (CNIC/PSID) that can't be captured as static text.
- For legal/official purposes, always cross-check current fees and requirements directly on dlims.punjab.gov.pk, as government fee schedules can be revised.
