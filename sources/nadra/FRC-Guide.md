# FRC — Family Registration Certificate

## 1. Overview

**FRC (Family Registration Certificate)** is a certificate issued by the **National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA)**.

It reflects, verifies, and provides a record of a registered person's **family composition data**. It is commonly used for immigration, visa processing, and other legal or administrative purposes.

---

## 2. Types of FRC

NADRA provides FRC according to the family relationship being documented:

### By Birth

Includes details of:

- Parents
- Siblings

### By Marriage

Includes details of:

- Spouse
- Children

### By Adoption

Includes details of:

- Guardian
- Adopted family members

### By All

Includes both:

- Family by birth
- Family by marriage

NADRA's current page describes the categories as **By Birth, By Marriage, By Adoption, and By All**.

---

## 3. Eligibility

A person can obtain an FRC if the relevant family members are registered with NADRA.

Generally:

- Family members should have a valid **13-digit NADRA identification number**.
- A registered family member can obtain an FRC showing the family's registered composition.
- In certain situations, unregistered deceased or living family members may also be included based on information provided by the applicant.

---

## 4. Requirements

The main requirement specified by NADRA is:

- **13-digit identification number of the applicant**, such as:
  - CNIC
  - NICOP
  - POC
  - CRC
  - Juvenile Card

Additional family information may be required depending on the type of FRC being requested.

### Important

The family information in the NADRA database should be correctly registered. If a spouse, child, parent, or other family member is missing from the NADRA family record, the relevant family record may need to be updated before the FRC can accurately reflect the family composition.

---

## 5. Application Methods

FRC can be obtained through NADRA's available registration and digital services.

Possible channels include:

1. **NADRA Registration Centre (NRC)**
2. **PakID Mobile App**
3. **Mobile Registration Vans (MRV)** where the service is available.

---

## 6. Applying Through PakID

### Step 1 — Open PakID

Open the official **NADRA PakID** mobile application.

### Step 2 — Sign in

Log in to your PakID account.

### Step 3 — Select FRC

Select the **Family Registration Certificate (FRC)** service.

### Step 4 — Select FRC Type

Choose the applicable family category:

- By Birth
- By Marriage
- By Adoption
- By All

### Step 5 — Enter / Confirm Information

Confirm the applicant's information and the family information available in the NADRA database.

### Step 6 — Submit Application

Review the information and submit the FRC application.

### Step 7 — Pay the Fee

Pay the applicable FRC fee through an available payment method.

NADRA supports payment methods including:

- Credit card
- Debit card
- Raast
- e-Sahulat
- Easypaisa
- JazzCash

e-Sahulat, Easypaisa and JazzCash are specifically listed as payment options for FRC applications.

### Step 8 — Track Application

The application can be tracked through the PakID application.

### Step 9 — Receive FRC

After approval, NADRA makes the FRC available in the **Applications** section of the PakID app.

---

## 7. Applying Through a NADRA Registration Centre

The general process is:

1. Visit a NADRA Registration Centre.
2. Request the **Family Registration Certificate** service.
3. Provide your 13-digit NADRA identification number.
4. Select the required FRC family category.
5. NADRA retrieves the registered family information.
6. Review the displayed information.
7. Pay the applicable fee.
8. Submit the application.
9. Receive the FRC according to the applicable processing procedure.

---

## 8. FRC Fees

According to NADRA's current fee structure:

| FRC Type | Fee | Processing Time |
|---|---:|---:|
| FRC — Alpha / Beta / Gama family | **PKR 1,000** | **1 day** |
| FRC — Both families | **PKR 2,000** | **1 day** |

The current NADRA fee table lists FRC under the **Executive** priority category, with no Normal or Urgent fee shown.

> **Note:** NADRA's terminology in the fee structure uses **Alpha / Beta / Gama family**, while the FRC information page describes the family categories as **By Birth, By Marriage, By Adoption, and By All**.

---

## 9. Processing Time

According to the current NADRA fee structure:

**FRC processing time: 1 day**

For:

- Alpha / Beta / Gama family — 1 day
- Both families — 1 day

---

## 10. What Information Does an FRC Show?

An FRC provides a record of the family composition maintained by NADRA.

Depending on the selected category, it can show relationships such as:

### By Birth

- Parents
- Brothers and sisters

### By Marriage

- Husband / wife
- Children

### By Adoption

- Guardian
- Adopted family members

### By All

- Family by birth
- Family by marriage

The certificate reflects the information available in NADRA's registration database.

---

## 11. Uses of FRC

FRC may be used for:

- Immigration applications
- Visa processing
- Family verification
- Government procedures
- Legal and administrative purposes
- Establishing family composition

NADRA specifically identifies immigration, visa processing, and other legal/administrative purposes as common uses.

---

## 12. Important Points

- FRC is a **family-composition certificate**, not a replacement for a CNIC.
- The information comes from NADRA's registered family data.
- A person must have the required NADRA identification information.
- Missing or incorrect family information may need to be corrected in NADRA's records.
- FRC can be requested for different family relationships.
- FRC does **not itself serve as proof of nationality**; it records family composition.
- NADRA's current fee structure lists a **1-day processing time** for FRC.
