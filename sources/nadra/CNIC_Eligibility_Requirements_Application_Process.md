# CNIC — Eligibility, Requirements, Application Process, Fees & Processing Time

## 1. Overview

The Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) is an official identity document issued by the National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA).

---

## 2. Eligibility

A CNIC is issued to Pakistani citizens who:

- Are **18 years of age or older**.
- Have not previously been registered with NADRA; or
- Are already registered as a holder of:
  - Child Registration Certificate (CRC / B-Form), or
  - Juvenile Card.

---

## 3. Required Documents

For a new CNIC, the applicant may be required to provide:

1. **Computerized Birth Certificate**
   - Verified birth certificate issued by the relevant:
     - Union Council
     - Municipal Committee
     - Cantonment Board

2. **Parent or Blood Relative's Identity Card**
   - CNIC/NICOP number of a parent or blood relative.

3. **Biometric Verification**
   - Biometric verification of a parent or blood relative.

4. **Citizenship/Naturalization Certificate**
   - Where applicable.

### Special Case

In certain cases where a blood relative is not available, NADRA may allow verification through an eligible biometric witness, subject to NADRA's requirements.

---

## 4. Application Methods

A new CNIC can be processed through a **NADRA Registration Centre**.

NADRA also provides the **PakID mobile application** for identity-document services.

### Important Note About First-Time CNIC

A first-time CNIC application may be initiated through an eligible blood relative's PakID account. The applicant and blood relative may then be required to visit a NADRA Registration Centre to complete the remaining verification steps.

---

# 5. Application Process at NADRA Registration Centre

### Step 1 — Visit NADRA

Visit a NADRA Registration Centre.

### Step 2 — Select New CNIC

Inform the NADRA representative that you want to apply for a **New CNIC**.

### Step 3 — Submit Documents

Provide the required supporting documents, such as:

- Verified computerized birth certificate
- Parent/blood-relative CNIC information
- Citizenship/naturalization certificate, where applicable

### Step 4 — Verification

NADRA verifies the applicant's information and family/identity records.

### Step 5 — Photograph and Biometrics

The applicant's required photograph and biometric information are captured.

### Step 6 — Review Information

Carefully check all information entered into the application, including:

- Name
- Father's name
- Date of birth
- Address
- Other personal information

### Step 7 — Confirmation

Confirm the application after checking that the information is correct.

### Step 8 — Pay the Applicable Fee

Select the required processing category and pay the applicable fee.

### Step 9 — Receive Application Details

Keep the receipt/application tracking information for checking the status of the application.

### Step 10 — Processing and Delivery

NADRA processes the application according to the selected priority category.

---

# 6. Applying Through PakID

PakID is NADRA's official mobile application for identity registration and tracking.

### General Process

1. Download the official **PakID** mobile application.
2. Create an account or sign in.
3. Select the relevant identity-card service.
4. Enter the required personal information.
5. Upload the required documents.
6. Complete biometric/photo requirements.
7. Visit a NADRA Registration Centre if required.
8. Pay the applicable fee.
9. Track the application through PakID.

---

# 7. CNIC Fees and Processing Time

According to the current NADRA fee structure:

| CNIC Service | Normal | Urgent | Executive |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **New CNIC** | Rs. 0 | Rs. 1,150 | Rs. 2,150 |
| **CNIC Modification** | Rs. 400 | Rs. 1,150 | Rs. 2,150 |
| **Duplicate CNIC** | Rs. 400 | Rs. 1,150 | Rs. 2,150 |
| **CNIC Renewal** | Rs. 400 | Rs. 1,150 | Rs. 2,150 |

### Processing Time

| Priority | Processing Time |
|---|---:|
| **Normal** | 15 days |
| **Urgent** | 12 days |
| **Executive** | 6 days |

---

# 9. Important Points

- CNIC eligibility begins at **18 years of age**.
- A verified computerized birth certificate is an important supporting document for a new CNIC.
- Parent/blood-relative identity information and biometric verification may be required.
- First-time CNIC applications have additional verification requirements.
- Keep the application receipt/tracking information after applying.
- Check all personal information carefully before confirming the application.
- Fees and processing times can change, so check the official NADRA fee structure before applying.
