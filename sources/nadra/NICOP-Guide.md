# NICOP — National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis

## 1. Overview

**NICOP** stands for **National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis**.

It is an identity document issued by the **National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA)** to Pakistani citizens residing outside Pakistan.

NICOP serves as official proof of Pakistani identity and nationality and allows eligible holders visa-free entry into Pakistan.

NADRA currently provides **Smart NICOP**, while new standard Teslin-based NICOP cards are no longer being issued.

---

## 2. Eligibility

A person may be eligible for NICOP if they fall into one of the following categories:

- Pakistani citizens living or working outside Pakistan.
- Pakistani citizens holding dual nationality of a country recognized by the Government of Pakistan.
- Pakistani citizens intending to stay abroad for an extended period who require proof of identity and nationality.
- Children born abroad to Pakistani citizens who are eligible to be registered as Pakistani citizens.

---

## 3. Required Documents

Depending on the applicant's circumstances, NADRA may require the following:

### Identity / Family Information

- Identity card number of a parent or blood relative.

OR

- Biometric witness by an ID-card holder together with the required **Undertaking "B"** where applicable.

### Birth / Citizenship Documents

For relevant applicants, one or more of the following may be required:

- Verified computerized birth certificate issued by:
  - Union Council (UC)
  - Municipal Committee
  - Cantonment
- Citizenship certificate.
- Naturalization certificate.
- Foreign detailed birth certificate.
- Foreign/manual birth certificate.
- S1 form issued by a Pakistani Embassy/Mission.

### Passport / Residence Documents

Depending on the case:

- Pakistani passport.
- Foreign passport.
- Residence permit.
- Travel document.
- Undertaking "A" duly attested by the Pakistani Embassy/Mission, where applicable.

### Biometric Verification

Biometric verification may be required through:

- Parent or blood relative.
- Attestation of CNICF under the applicable regulations for certain inland applications.
- Verification of the application form by an eligible ID-card holder for applicants applying from abroad.

> **Important:** The exact supporting documents depend on the applicant's individual circumstances. NADRA may request additional documentation or verification.

---

## 4. Application Methods

NICOP can be applied for through:

1. **NADRA PakID mobile application**
2. **NADRA Registration Centre**, where applicable.

---

## 5. Applying Through the PakID App

### Step 1 — Download PakID

Download and install the official **NADRA PakID** mobile application.

### Step 2 — Create / Access Your Account

- Create a PakID account, or
- Sign in to an existing account.

### Step 3 — Select NICOP

Select:

**New NICOP**

### Step 4 — Enter Personal Information

Provide the requested information, such as:

- Personal details
- Family information
- Citizenship information
- Contact information
- Overseas address

### Step 5 — Upload Documents

Upload the required supporting documents according to your application category.

### Step 6 — Complete Verification

NADRA may require:

- Photograph
- Biometric verification
- Additional verification

If required, the applicant may need to visit a NADRA Registration Centre.

### Step 7 — Pay the Fee

Pay the applicable NICOP processing fee according to the selected processing priority and region.

### Step 8 — Submit Application

Review the information and submit the application.

### Step 9 — Track Application

The application status can be tracked through the PakID system.

---

## 6. Applying Through a NADRA Registration Centre

The general process is:

1. Visit the relevant NADRA Registration Centre.
2. Select the NICOP service.
3. Provide the required documents.
4. Provide personal and family information.
5. Complete biometric verification where required.
6. Have your photograph taken where required.
7. Pay the applicable processing fee.
8. Receive the application/receipt information.
9. Track the application and receive the NICOP according to the applicable delivery procedure.

---

## 8. Processing Time

According to NADRA's current fee structure:

| Priority | Processing Time |
|---|---:|
| Normal | 31 days |
| Urgent | 23 days |
| Executive | 9 days |

These timelines are listed for Smart NICOP applications.

---

## 9. NICOP Validity

According to NADRA's current information:

- **Adult Smart NICOP:** 10 years
- **Minor Smart NICOP:** 10 years
- **Minor below 14 years:** 10 years or until reaching 18 years, whichever comes first.

---

## 10. NICOP Services Available

NADRA provides services including:

- New NICOP
- NICOP Renewal
- NICOP Modification
- NICOP Duplicate / Reprint
- Lost NICOP replacement
- Updating certain non-printable fields
- NICOP cancellation in applicable circumstances

---

## 11. Important Points

- NICOP is intended for **Pakistani citizens residing overseas**.
- Children born abroad to Pakistani citizens may also qualify if they are eligible for Pakistani citizenship.
- The exact documents required can vary depending on the applicant's circumstances.
- NADRA may require biometric verification or additional documentation.
- New standard Teslin-based NICOP cards are currently not being issued; NADRA's current service information focuses on Smart NICOP.
- NADRA has announced that the QR-code-based identity-card system will be extended to NICOP from **January 2027**. Existing cards remain valid until their expiry dates.
