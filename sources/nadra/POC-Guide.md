# POC — Pakistan Origin Card

## 1. Overview

**POC (Pakistan Origin Card)** is an identity document issued by the **National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA)** to foreign nationals who have Pakistani origins or family ties to Pakistan.

POC allows eligible foreign nationals to:

- Enter Pakistan without a visa.
- Stay in Pakistan indefinitely.
- Purchase and own property (subject to applicable laws).
- Open bank accounts.
- Work and conduct business in Pakistan.

POC is issued as a **Smart Pakistan Origin Card**.

---

## 2. Eligibility

A person may apply for a POC if they fall into one of the following categories:

### A. Former Pakistani Citizens

- Individuals who were previously Pakistani citizens and later acquired foreign nationality.

### B. Pakistani Descent

- Children of Pakistani citizens.
- Grandchildren of Pakistani citizens.
- Great-grandchildren of Pakistani citizens.

### C. Foreign Spouse

- Foreign husband or wife of a Pakistani citizen.

### D. Other Persons of Pakistani Origin

- Foreign nationals who can provide documentary proof of Pakistani ancestry.

---

## 3. Required Documents

Required documents vary according to the applicant category.

### For Former Pakistani Citizens

- Previous CNIC or NICOP (if available).
- Pakistani passport (if available).
- Current foreign passport.
- Foreign nationality certificate (if applicable).

### For Children / Descendants

- Foreign passport.
- Birth certificate.
- Parent's or grandparent's CNIC/NICOP.
- Family relationship documents.

### For Foreign Spouse

- Valid foreign passport.
- Marriage certificate.
- Pakistani spouse's CNIC/NICOP.
- Additional verification documents if required.

### General Documents

- Recent photograph.
- Biometric verification (where required).
- Supporting family relationship documents.

> NADRA may request additional documents depending on the case.

---

## 4. Application Methods

POC applications can be submitted through:

1. **PakID Mobile App**
2. **NADRA Registration Centre**
3. **Pakistani Embassy / Consulate** (where applicable)

---

## 5. Applying Through PakID App

### Step 1: Download PakID

Download the official NADRA PakID mobile application.

### Step 2: Create Account

- Register a new account, or
- Sign in to an existing account.

### Step 3: Select Service

Choose:

**New POC**

### Step 4: Enter Information

Provide:

- Personal details
- Passport details
- Family information
- Contact information

### Step 5: Upload Documents

Upload the required supporting documents.

### Step 6: Verification

NADRA may require:

- Photograph
- Biometrics
- Additional verification

A visit to a NADRA Registration Centre may be required in some cases.

### Step 7: Pay Fee

Pay the applicable fee according to selected priority.

### Step 8: Submit

Review and submit the application.

### Step 9: Track Status

Track the application through the PakID system.

---

## 6. Applying Through NADRA Registration Centre

1. Visit NADRA Registration Centre.
2. Select POC service.
3. Submit required documents.
4. Complete biometrics.
5. Capture photograph.
6. Pay fee.
7. Receive tracking receipt.
8. Collect POC after processing.

| Priority | Processing Time |
|-----------|----------------|
| Normal | 31 Days |
| Urgent | 23 Days |
| Executive | 09 Days |

Cancellation services are generally processed in approximately 2 days.

---

## 9. POC Validity

POC is generally issued with long-term validity. Specific validity periods may depend on the applicant category and current NADRA policies. Applicants should verify validity at the time of application.

---

## 10. Benefits of POC

POC holders can:

- Enter Pakistan without a visa.
- Stay in Pakistan indefinitely.
- Open bank accounts.
- Purchase and own property.
- Work and conduct business.
- Obtain a driving licence (subject to regulations).
