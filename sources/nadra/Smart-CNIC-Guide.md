# Smart CNIC — Eligibility, Requirements, Application Process, Fees & Processing

The following is based on the official NADRA information currently available. NADRA lists Smart CNIC as a national identity-card service and provides separate fees and processing times for new cards, modifications, duplicates, and renewals.

## 1. What is a Smart CNIC?

A Smart CNIC (Smart National Identity Card) is NADRA's secure, chip-based national identity card for eligible Pakistani citizens.

It is an identity document issued by NADRA and can be applied for through the relevant NADRA channels.

## 2. Eligibility

A Smart CNIC is intended for Pakistani citizens who are eligible for an adult national identity card.

Generally, this means:

- The applicant is a Pakistani citizen.
- The applicant is 18 years of age or older for an adult identity card.
- The applicant is registered or eligible for registration in NADRA's national identity database.

For a first-time adult registration, NADRA may require additional family and identity verification.

## 3. Required Documents

The exact documents can depend on the type of application and the applicant's NADRA record.

For a first-time adult identity registration, commonly required supporting information includes:

- Computerized birth certificate
- Parent's or blood relative's CNIC/NICOP information
- Required biometric verification
- Citizenship/naturalization documentation where applicable
- Other supporting documents requested by NADRA for a particular case

NADRA may require additional verification if the applicant's information cannot be verified automatically.

## 4. Types of Smart CNIC Applications

NADRA currently lists these Smart CNIC services:

- New Smart CNIC
- Smart CNIC Renewal
- Smart CNIC Modification
- Smart CNIC Duplicate/Reprint

These services have the same fee structure but differ depending on the application type.

## 5. Application Process

### A. Through a NADRA Registration Centre

**Step 1 — Visit NADRA**

Visit a NADRA Registration Centre.

**Step 2 — Select the Required Service**

Tell the NADRA representative that you want to apply for:

- New Smart CNIC
- Smart CNIC Renewal
- Smart CNIC Modification
- Smart CNIC Duplicate/Reprint

**Step 3 — Provide Documents**

Submit the required documents and information.

**Step 4 — Verification**

NADRA verifies your identity and information against its records.

**Step 5 — Photograph and Biometrics**

NADRA captures the required photograph and biometric information.

**Step 6 — Review Your Information**

Carefully check the information displayed by the NADRA officer.

Check details such as:

- Name
- Father's name
- Date of birth
- Address
- Other personal information

**Step 7 — Confirm the Application**

Confirm the information after ensuring that it is correct.

**Step 8 — Pay the Fee**

Pay the fee corresponding to your selected processing priority.

**Step 9 — Keep Your Receipt**

Keep your application receipt/tracking information.

**Step 10 — Card Processing**

NADRA processes and issues the Smart CNIC according to the selected priority category.

## 6. Application Through PakID

NADRA's PakID mobile application provides digital identity-document services. NADRA describes PakID as its official mobile application for identity registration and tracking.

The general process is:

1. Download/open the official PakID application.
2. Create an account or sign in.
3. Select the relevant identity-card service.
4. Enter your personal information.
5. Upload the required documents.
6. Complete the required photograph/biometric steps.
7. Visit a NADRA Registration Centre if NADRA requires physical verification.
8. Pay the applicable fee.
9. Track the application.

NADRA's PakID information notes that biometric verification and photography may require a visit to a NADRA Registration Centre.

## 7. Smart CNIC Fees

According to NADRA's current fee structure:

| Smart CNIC Service | Normal | Urgent | Executive |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Smart CNIC | Rs. 750 | Rs. 1,500 | Rs. 2,500 |
| Smart CNIC Modification | Rs. 750 | Rs. 1,500 | Rs. 2,500 |
| Smart CNIC Duplicate | Rs. 750 | Rs. 1,500 | Rs. 2,500 |
| Smart CNIC Renewal | Rs. 750 | Rs. 1,500 | Rs. 2,500 |

These are the fees currently displayed on NADRA's official fee-structure page.

## 8. Processing Time

| Priority | Processing Time |
|---|---|
| Normal | 31 days |
| Urgent | 23 days |
| Executive | 9 days |

The same processing-time categories currently apply to new Smart CNIC, modification, duplicate, and renewal applications.

## 9. Validity

NADRA's current fee-structure information lists the validity of an adult CNIC/Smart CNIC/NICOP as 10 years.

## 10. Payment

For applications through PakID, NADRA provides several payment methods. Its payment guidelines include options such as:

- Credit/debit card
- Easypaisa
- JazzCash
- e-Sahulat
- Raast

For Smart NIC and FRC payments, NADRA's payment instructions also describe payment through a banking-app QR process.

## 11. Important Notes

- Smart CNIC is different from a normal CNIC; NADRA has a separate fee structure for it.
- The current fee for a new Smart CNIC is Rs. 750 under Normal processing.
- Normal processing is currently listed as 31 days.
- Urgent processing is 23 days.
- Executive processing is 9 days.
- Requirements can vary depending on whether the application is new, renewal, modification, or duplicate.
- NADRA may request additional documents or verification depending on the applicant's record.
- Fees and processing times can change, so the official NADRA fee page should be checked before submitting an application.
