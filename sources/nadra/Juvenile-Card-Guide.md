# Juvenile Card — Eligibility, Requirements, Application Process, Fees & Processing

## 1. Overview

The **Juvenile Card** is an identity document issued by the **National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA)** to Pakistani citizens who are **under 18 years of age**.

It provides proof of the child's identity and relationship with their parents or legal guardian.

The Juvenile Card is issued under **Rule 18 of the NADRA (NIC) Rules, 2002**.

---

## 2. Eligibility

According to NADRA, every Pakistani citizen who has **not attained the age of 18 years** is eligible for registration with NADRA through a **Child Registration Certificate (CRC) or Juvenile Card**.

### Basic eligibility

The applicant must:

- Be a Pakistani citizen.
- Be under 18 years of age.
- Be registered or eligible for registration in the NADRA database.

---

## 3. Required Documents

The main requirement for a Juvenile Card application is proof of the child's birth and identity.

### Required documents

- **Computerized Birth Registration Certificate** issued by:
  - Union Council
  - Municipal Committee
  - Cantonment

For a child born outside Pakistan, one of the applicable documents may be required:

- Detailed Birth Certificate
- S-1 Form issued by the Pakistani Embassy/Mission

### Other supporting information

NADRA may require information or documentation establishing the child's relationship with the parents or legal guardian.

---

## 4. Child's Presence

The child's physical presence is **mandatory when applying for a Juvenile Card**.

This allows NADRA to capture the required photograph and biometrics.

---

## 5. Biometrics

The biometric requirements depend on the child's age.

| Age | Photograph | Fingerprints | Iris |
|---|---|---|---|
| Up to 10 years | Yes | No | Yes |
| 10–18 years | Yes | Yes | Yes |

The Juvenile Card expires when the child reaches the applicable age milestone.

According to NADRA's current information:

- Juvenile Card issued to a child under 10 years is valid until the child reaches **10 years**.
- Juvenile Card issued at age 10 or above is valid until the child reaches **18 years**.

---

## 6. Application Methods

A new Juvenile Card can be applied for through:

1. **NADRA PakID App**
2. **NADRA Registration Centre**

---

## 7. Application Through PakID App

### Step 1 — Download PakID

Download the official **NADRA PakID mobile application**.

### Step 2 — Create or Sign In

- Create a PakID account, or
- Sign in to an existing account.

### Step 3 — Select Juvenile Card

Select:

**New Juvenile Card**

### Step 4 — Enter Information

Provide the required information about the child, including:

- Name
- Date of birth
- Parent information
- Family information
- Other required personal details

### Step 5 — Upload Documents

Upload the required supporting documents, particularly the child's birth registration document.

### Step 6 — Verification / NADRA Centre Visit

If required, visit a NADRA Registration Centre for:

- Photograph
- Fingerprints
- Iris capture
- Other verification

### Step 7 — Pay the Fee

Pay the applicable NADRA processing fee according to the selected priority.

### Step 8 — Submit Application

Review the information and submit the application.

### Step 9 — Track Application

Track the application through the PakID application.

---

## 8. Application Through NADRA Registration Centre

The general procedure is:

1. Visit a NADRA Registration Centre.
2. Select the Juvenile Card service.
3. Bring the child's required birth documentation.
4. Ensure the child is present.
5. Provide the required personal and family information.
6. Complete photograph and biometric requirements.
7. Pay the applicable processing fee.
8. Receive the application/receipt information.
9. Track or receive the card according to NADRA's delivery procedure.

---

## 9. Fees and Processing Time

NADRA's current fee structure lists the following processing priorities for its identity-document services:

| Priority | Processing Time |
|---|---:|
| Normal | 15 days |
| Urgent | 12 days |
| Executive | 6 days |

For the current fee applicable to a particular Juvenile Card transaction, applicants should check NADRA's official fee structure at the time of application because fees can be revised.

**Official NADRA Fee Structure:**
https://www.nadra.gov.pk/feeStructure

---

## 10. Validity

The Juvenile Card has age-based validity:

| Applicant Age | Validity |
|---|---|
| Under 10 years | Until attaining 10 years |
| 10–17 years | Until attaining 18 years |

After reaching adulthood, the individual becomes eligible for an adult **CNIC / Smart CNIC**, subject to NADRA's requirements.

---

## 11. Juvenile Card vs CRC

Both documents are used for children under 18, but they are different identity documents.

| Feature | CRC / B-Form | Juvenile Card |
|---|---|---|
| Full name | Child Registration Certificate | Juvenile Card |
| Eligible age | Under 18 | Under 18 |
| Physical card | No | Yes |
| Photograph | Age-dependent | Yes |
| Fingerprints | Age-dependent | Required from age 10 |
| Iris | Age-dependent | Required |
| Purpose | Child registration | Child identity document |

---

## 12. Important Points

- The child must be a Pakistani citizen.
- The child must be under 18.
- The child's presence is mandatory for a Juvenile Card application.
- A computerized birth registration certificate is an important supporting document.
- For children born abroad, a detailed birth certificate or applicable S-1 form may be required.
- Biometric requirements vary according to age.
- NADRA may request additional documentation or verification in individual cases.
- Existing identity cards remain valid until their stated expiry dates.
- NADRA has announced that QR-code-based identity cards will be introduced for Juvenile Cards from **January 2027**. Existing cards remain valid until expiry.
