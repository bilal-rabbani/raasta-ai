# SECP Online Services / eServices

## Overview
SECP has fully digitized most of its corporate registration and filing functions through two related web platforms:
- **eZfile** (https://leap.secp.gov.pk) — SECP's current, modernized online filing portal for name reservation, incorporation, post-incorporation filings, and licensing-related applications.
- **eServices** (https://eservices.secp.gov.pk) — the earlier online portal, still used for certain functions such as the free public **Company Name Search** tool, and referenced in older user-registration guidance.

## What Can Be Done Online
- Company name reservation and search
- Company incorporation (all types: SMC, private, public, guarantee/Section 42)
- LLP name reservation and incorporation
- Filing of statutory forms (Form A/B annual returns, Form 29, Form 45, LLP forms, etc.)
- Filing of changes (registered office, directors, share capital, memorandum/articles)
- Payment of fees (via system-generated challan, debit/credit card, or 1-Bill)
- Tracking status of submitted applications/processes
- Accessing user account management (update profile, view submitted processes, manage attached documents)

## User Registration Process
1. Visit the eServices/eZfile portal.
2. Click "Sign Up"/"User Registration" and provide required details (CNIC or passport number, mobile number, email address).
3. Pay a one-time, nominal **user registration fee** (commonly cited around **PKR 100**), charged at the time of first process submission.
4. Verify email/mobile as prompted, then log in to access the "Processes List" — the home page listing all available SECP processes the user can initiate.

## Key Functional Features (eServices Account Dashboard)
- **Processes List** — access point for all available eServices processes (name reservation, incorporation, statutory filings, etc.).
- **Update Profile** — manage account details.
- **Submitted Processes** — track processes currently under action or already completed.
- **Forms** — view previously attached forms/documents.
- **Secondary/Intermediary Users** — add or delete additional authorized users (e.g., company secretaries, consultants) linked to the primary account, each with defined access.
- **Logout** — securely exit the running session.

## eZfile-Specific Notes
- eZfile is positioned as SECP's newer, more streamlined system, and incorporation/annual filing processes are increasingly routed through it rather than the legacy eServices interface.
- An **eZfile User Manual** (covering name reservation and incorporation, and separately for post-incorporation filings) is published on SECP's website as a step-by-step reference.
- SECP has also published short instructional videos covering **User Registration**, **Company Incorporation**, and **Annual Filing** via eZfile, available through SECP's official Facebook page and website media center.
- An **Offline Application Helper** is available for generating the manual challan used when filing name reservation/incorporation applications in physical form rather than through eZfile.

## Technical Requirements
- A personal computer (or compatible device) with a modern web browser.
- An internet connection (broadband recommended).
- A valid CNIC (Pakistani nationals) or passport (foreign nationals) for identity verification during registration.

## Related Specialized Portals
- **Startup Portal** (startups.secp.gov.pk) — resources and guidance targeted at new/startup businesses.
- **SDMS / SECP-XS** — SECP's complaint and service-request management systems (see the Complaints file for details); SDMS is being phased out in favor of **SECP-XS** (xs.secp.gov.pk).
