# SECP Complaints and Contact Information

## Overview
SECP provides multiple channels for lodging complaints and queries — ranging from a dedicated online complaint/service-desk system to physical drop boxes, email, and a toll-free helpline. SECP is transitioning its complaint infrastructure from the older **Service Desk Management System (SDMS)** to a new platform called **SECP-XS**.

## Primary Complaint Channels

### 1. SECP-XS (New/Current Platform)
- SECP is phasing out SDMS and directing stakeholders to **SECP-XS**, its "new and improved support platform," for submitting requests and accessing related services.
- Portal: https://xs.secp.gov.pk/

### 2. SDMS – Service Desk Management System (Being Phased Out)
- Still operational during the transition period.
- Portal: https://sdms.secp.gov.pk/
- Call timings: **Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 5:00 pm** (lunch break 1:00 pm–2:00 pm).
- After 5 pm and on holidays, the SDMS portal (or SECP-XS) should be the first choice for lodging a complaint/query rather than calling.
- Complaint categories handled include: capital markets and intermediaries (CDC, NCCPL, PSX, PMEX, brokers/agents), listed-company trading issues, insider trading/market manipulation/rumors, insurance companies/brokers/agents/surveyors, and NBFC-related matters (asset management companies, mutual funds, pension funds, investment advisors, REITs, leasing companies, investment banks, microfinance companies, housing finance companies).

### 3. Email
- General complaints: **complaints@secp.gov.pk**
- General queries: **queries@secp.gov.pk**

## What SECP's Complaint Process Covers
Per SECP's Complaints & Service Desk Guide, a valid complaint must arise from activities covered under: the SECP Act, 1997; the Companies Act, 2017 (and the earlier Companies Ordinance, 1984 where still relevant); the Securities Act, 2015; the Insurance Ordinance, 2000; the Central Depository Companies Act, 1997; and rules/regulations made thereunder — relating to entities SECP regulates (listed companies, stock brokers/sub-brokers, stock exchanges, depository/depository participants, share registrars/transfer agents, mutual funds/NBFCs, insurance companies, etc.).
