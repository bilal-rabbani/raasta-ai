# SECP Licensing and Regulated Entities (Overview)

## Overview
Beyond ordinary company registration, SECP acts as the **licensing and prudential regulator** for a wide range of specialized entities operating in Pakistan's corporate, financial, insurance, and capital markets sectors. A business intending to operate in any of the categories below cannot rely on plain company incorporation alone — it must additionally obtain a specific **license/registration certificate** from the relevant SECP division before commencing that regulated activity. (Insurance, NBFCs, and Capital Markets each have their own dedicated files in this set with full eligibility/process/fee detail — this file gives the cross-cutting picture and the categories not covered elsewhere.)

## Broad Licensing Categories at SECP
1. **Capital Markets** — stock exchanges, commodity exchanges, depository companies, clearing houses, securities brokers/agents, debt securities trustees, share registrars/balloters, underwriters/consultants/bankers to the issue, credit rating companies, special purpose vehicles, securities & futures advisors.
2. **NBFCs (Non-Banking Finance Companies)** — asset management companies, PE & VC fund management companies, investment advisors, leasing companies, housing finance companies, investment finance companies, voluntary pension fund managers, modarabas, REIT management companies.
3. **Insurance Sector** — life/non-life insurers, takaful/window takaful operators, insurance brokers, insurance surveyors, authorized surveying officers, third-party administrators.
4. **Section 42 Companies** — associations not for profit (NGOs, trade bodies, charitable/educational entities) licensed to omit "Limited" from their name and operate without a profit motive.

## Section 42 Companies (Not-for-Profit Associations)
### Eligibility
- Formed (or intended to be formed) for promoting commerce, art, science, religion, sport, social services, charity, or any other useful object.
- Must apply the entity's profits/income, if any, solely towards promoting its objects.
- Must prohibit payment of dividends to members.

### Process
1. Apply to SECP for a license under Section 42 before or alongside incorporation, providing the proposed objects, draft memorandum/articles, and a declaration regarding non-distribution of profits.
2. SECP reviews the application (including scrutiny of promoters and objects) and, if satisfied, grants a Section 42 license.
3. The company is then incorporated as a "company limited by guarantee" (or company limited by shares, as applicable) and may use a name without "Limited"/"(Guarantee) Limited" suffix, subject to license conditions.

## General Principles Applicable Across SECP-Licensed Sectors
- **Fit and Proper Test:** Promoters, sponsors, directors, and key executives of licensed entities (NBFCs, insurers, brokers, etc.) must satisfy SECP's "fit and proper" criteria covering integrity, financial soundness, competence, and track record.
- **Minimum Capital / Statutory Deposit Requirements:** Most licensed categories carry a prescribed minimum paid-up capital and, in several cases (insurance, NBFCs), a statutory deposit with the State Bank of Pakistan.
- **NOC-then-Incorporation-then-License Sequence:** For most specialized licenses (NBFCs, insurance), the typical sequence is: (a) obtain a No Objection Certificate (NOC)/in-principle approval from the relevant SECP department; (b) incorporate the entity as a public or private limited company with SECP's Company Registration Office; (c) apply for and obtain the final operating license from the relevant licensing division.
- **Ongoing Supervision:** Licensed entities are subject to periodic reporting (quarterly/annual), inspections, and can have licenses suspended, cancelled, or not renewed for non-compliance with the governing ordinance/rules/regulations.
- **Renewal:** Many licenses (e.g., securities broker licenses) are valid for a fixed term (commonly one year) and must be renewed with updated fit-and-proper and financial-soundness documentation.
