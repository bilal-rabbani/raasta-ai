# SECP Forms and Applications

## Overview
SECP maintains a structured set of statutory forms and applications under the Companies Act, 2017, the LLP Act, 2017, and various sector-specific regulations. Most forms can be filed electronically through **eZfile** (leap.secp.gov.pk) or, where physical filing is retained, in paper form at a Company Registration Office (CRO), accompanied by the prescribed fee (paid via eZfile-generated challan, credit/debit card, 1-Bill, or designated bank branches for manual challans).

## Main Categories of Forms (as organized on SECP's website)

### 1. Applications (Pre-Incorporation / Name & Incorporation Stage)
- Name reservation application (combined or separate from incorporation)
- Incorporation application (Memorandum & Articles of Association submission)
- Applications for licenses under Section 42 (not-for-profit companies)
- Applications for conversion of company type (e.g., private to public, or vice versa)

### 2. Statutory Forms (Post-Incorporation / Ongoing Compliance)
Common statutory forms include (illustrative, not exhaustive — exact form numbers/names should be confirmed against the current SECP Statutory Forms list, as numbering has evolved across the Companies Ordinance, 1984 and Companies Act, 2017 regimes):
- **Form A** — Annual return (company having share capital)
- **Form B** — Annual return (company not having share capital)
- **Form 29** — Particulars of directors, chief executive, secretary, and other officers, and changes therein
- **Form 45** — Annual return of a foreign company (list of Pakistani members/debenture holders and places of business)
- **Form 28** — (referenced alongside Forms 29/45 in filings following elections of directors — used for filings tied to changes at incorporation/registered office, per practitioner guidance)
- Forms for change of registered office address, alteration of share capital, change of company name, and other "Changes in Company" filings

### 3. Post-Incorporation Statutory Filing Schedule
SECP publishes a schedule indicating which forms must be filed, within what timeframe, following specific triggering events (e.g., appointment of first directors, allotment of shares, creation/modification/satisfaction of charges/mortgages, changes in memorandum/articles).

### 4. Statutory Forms for Foreign Companies
A separate set of forms applies to foreign companies establishing a place of business in Pakistan, covering:
- Initial registration/establishment of a place of business
- Annual filing of accounts and Form 45 (list of Pakistani members/debenture holders)
- Notification of changes in directors, registered office (in the home jurisdiction and in Pakistan), or charter documents

### 5. LLP Forms (Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2017)
- **LLP Form I** — Application for reservation of name
- **LLP Form II** — Application for change or rectification of name
- **LLP Form III** — Application for incorporation of LLP
- **LLP Form IV** — Consent to act as partner or designated partner
- **LLP Form V** — Notice of induction and cessation of partners
- **LLP Form VI** — Application for conversion of firm or company to LLP
- **LLP Form VII** — Change of registered address
- **LLP Form VIII** — Change of LLP Agreement
- **LLP Form IX** — Notice to partners for providing particulars of ultimate beneficial owners
- **LLP Form X** — Declaration by partner about ultimate beneficial owners
- **LLP Form XI** — Declaration by partner about change of ultimate beneficial owners
- **LLP Form XII** — Declaration of compliance with Section 8 of the LLP Act, 2017

### 6. Manual (Offline) Challan
For applicants who cannot or prefer not to file electronically, SECP provides:
- A **Challan Guide** explaining how to complete the manual payment challan
- The **Challan** itself (downloadable/generatable form) for depositing fees at designated bank branches for offline name reservation and incorporation

## How to Access and Submit Forms
1. **Electronic filing (preferred):** Register on **eZfile** (leap.secp.gov.pk), select the relevant process/form from the process list, complete the online form, attach required documents (scanned/digital), and pay the fee via the system-generated challan or card payment.
2. **Manual/offline filing:** Download the relevant form/challan from the SECP website, complete it, pay the fee at a designated bank, and submit the physical form with attachments at the concerned Company Registration Office.
