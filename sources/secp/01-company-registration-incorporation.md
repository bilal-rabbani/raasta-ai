# SECP Company Registration / Incorporation

## Overview
Company incorporation in Pakistan is regulated by the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) under the **Companies Act, 2017** and the **Companies Regulations, 2024**. Incorporation is end-to-end digitized through SECP's online filing portal, **eZfile** (leap.secp.gov.pk), which replaced the older eServices portal for new filings. Registration can also be done in physical/manual form at any of SECP's nine **Company Registration Offices (CROs)** in Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore, Multan, Peshawar, Sukkur, Faisalabad, Quetta, and Gilgit.

## Types of Companies That Can Be Incorporated
- **Single Member Company (SMC-Private Limited)** — one person only
- **Private Limited Company** — 2 to 50 members
- **Public Unlisted Company** — 3 or more members
- **Public Listed Company** — 3 or more members, shares traded on a stock exchange
- **Company Limited by Guarantee** (not-for-profit, includes Section 42 companies)
- **Foreign Company** (branch/liaison office of a foreign entity establishing a place of business in Pakistan)
- **Association Not for Profit** licensed under Section 42 of the Companies Act, 2017

## Eligibility
- **Public company:** any three (3) or more persons associated for a lawful purpose, subscribing their names to the Memorandum of Association (MOA).
- **Private company:** any two (2) or more persons.
- **Single Member Company:** one natural person (with a nominee director in case of death/incapacity).
- Directors/subscribers must have a valid CNIC (for Pakistani nationals) or passport (for foreign nationals). Foreign nationals and foreign body-corporates may be shareholders and directors; where a foreign body corporate is a subscriber, apostilled/legalized constitutional documents and a board resolution are required.
- Every company must have a registered office address in Pakistan (owned, rented, or used with proper consent — a residential address may qualify if verifiable).
- A private limited company/SMC requires a minimum of one director; other structures follow the director-count rules set out in the Companies Act, 2017 and Regulations.

## Step-by-Step Incorporation Process
1. **Name Reservation** — Reserve a unique company name via eZfile (see the separate Name Reservation file for detailed criteria). A combined name-reservation-and-incorporation application can also be filed as a single request.
2. **Document Preparation** — Draft the Memorandum of Association (MOA) and Articles of Association (AOA); SECP provides standard/model templates (including "Short Articles of Association") on its portal that can be adapted to the company's objects.
3. **Subscriber/Director Details** — Provide CNICs/passports, addresses, shareholding pattern, and consent-to-act forms for directors.
4. **Online Submission via eZfile** — Upload the incorporation application, MOA/AOA, registered office address proof, and pay the prescribed fee (see the Fees file). The process is described by SECP as comprising four simple, user-friendly digital steps.
5. **Scrutiny by the Registrar** — The Company Registration Office concerned reviews the application for compliance with the Act and Regulations. Objections (if any) are communicated for correction.
6. **Certificate of Incorporation** — Once approved, SECP issues the electronic Certificate of Incorporation, and the company obtains its Corporate Universal Identification Number (CUIN).
7. **Post-Incorporation Steps** — Obtain an NTN from FBR, activate the tax profile, open a corporate bank account, and comply with post-incorporation statutory filings (Form 29 for first directors/officers, etc.).

## Documents Typically Required
- Reserved name letter/CUIN of name approval
- Memorandum and Articles of Association (signed by subscribers)
- CNICs of Pakistani subscribers/directors; passports of foreign subscribers/directors
- Registered office address proof
- Consent-to-act letters from directors
- Board resolution and apostilled/legalized documents (where a foreign company/body corporate is a subscriber)
- NOC from the concerned ministry/regulator, where the objects require prior clearance (e.g., banking, insurance, security services, etc.)

## Processing Time
- Name reservation: typically decided within 1–3 working days (name valid for a limited period, commonly cited as up to 60 days, after which it must be renewed if incorporation is not completed).
- Incorporation: standard/local incorporation is often completed within a few working days to about a week once a complete application is filed; foreign-linked or complex cases may take longer.

## Governing Legal Framework
- Companies Act, 2017
- Companies Regulations, 2024 (available on SECP's website, updated periodically)
- Seventh Schedule to the Companies Act, 2017 (fee schedule)
