# SECP Rules, Regulations, Notifications and Laws

## Overview
SECP's regulatory output is organized on its website into a structured hierarchy of legal instruments: Acts, Ordinances, Rules, Regulations, Notifications, Directives, Guidelines, Circulars, Drafts for Discussion, and Others. Together these form the complete legal framework governing companies, LLPs, capital markets, NBFCs, and the insurance sector in Pakistan.

## Categories of Legal Instruments (as organized by SECP)

### 1. Acts
Primary legislation enacted by Parliament, forming the foundation of SECP's mandate and the regimes it administers. Key Acts include:
- **Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan Act, 1997** — establishes SECP itself.
- **Companies Act, 2017** — the principal law governing incorporation, governance, and regulation of companies (superseding the earlier Companies Ordinance, 1984 for most purposes).
- **Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2017**
- **Securities Act, 2015**
- **Futures Market Act, 2016**
- **Stock Exchanges (Corporatization, Demutualization and Integration) Act, 2012**
- **Central Depositories Act, 1997**
- **Anti-Money Laundering Act, 2010**

### 2. Ordinances
Instruments promulgated with the force of law (some later replaced/superseded, but still referenced for transitional or historical matters), including:
- **Companies Ordinance, 1984** (largely replaced by the Companies Act, 2017, but still relevant for certain transitional provisions and for entities like insurance companies where cross-references remain)
- **Modaraba (Floatation and Control) Ordinance, 1980**
- **Insurance Ordinance, 2000** (the core insurance-sector law)

### 3. Rules
Subordinate legislation made under an Act/Ordinance, detailing operational requirements. Key rules include:
- **Companies (General Provisions & Forms) Rules, 1985**
- **Companies Rules, 1996**
- **Non-Banking Finance Companies (Establishment and Regulation) Rules, 2003**
- **Insurance Rules, 2017** (and earlier 2002 rules, as amended)
- **Takaful Rules, 2012**
- **Brokers and Agents Registration Rules, 2001**
- **Commodity Exchange and Future Contracts Rules**
- **Companies (Audit of Cost Accounts) Rules, 1998**

### 4. Regulations
More detailed, frequently updated instruments covering specific processes/sectors:
- **Companies Regulations, 2024** — the current operative regulations detailing incorporation, filing, and governance procedures under the Companies Act, 2017 (periodically updated; check for the latest "updated upto" date on SECP's site).
- **Limited Liability Partnership Regulations, 2018**
- **Non-Banking Finance Companies and Notified Entities Regulations, 2008**
- **Securities Brokers (Licensing and Operations) Regulations, 2016**
- **Public Offering Regulations, 2017** (updated periodically — most recently referenced update: May 25, 2026)
- **Private Fund Regulations, 2015**
- **Debt Securities Trustee Regulations, 2012**
- **The Insurance Companies (Sound and Prudent Management) Regulations, 2012**
- **Securities and Exchange Commission (Re-insurance Brokers) Regulations, 2021**
- **Companies (Easy Exit) Regulations, 2014**

### 5. Notifications
Statutory Regulatory Orders (S.R.O.s) issued by SECP to amend rules/regulations, announce fee changes, or implement policy decisions — e.g., S.R.O.s revising the Seventh Schedule fee table, or amending the Companies Regulations, 2024.

### 6. Directives, Guidelines, and Circulars
- **Directives** — binding instructions issued to regulated entities on specific compliance matters.
- **Guidelines** — non-binding but authoritative interpretive guidance (e.g., Guidelines for Company Name Reservation).
- **Circulars** — used for procedural/administrative updates, such as fee revisions (e.g., Circular No. 13 of 2024 on Seventh Schedule fee increases) or disclosure requirements for specific licensees (e.g., disclosure requirements for pension fund managers).

### 7. Drafts for Discussion
SECP publishes proposed draft laws, rules, and regulations for public/stakeholder comment before finalization, along with a record of public comments received.

### 8. Orders (Enforcement)
A distinct but related category — orders issued by SECP under various Acts/Ordinances/Rules in the course of enforcement action against specific companies or licensees (e.g., orders under Section 132 of the Companies Act, 2017, or under the Anti-Money Laundering Act, 2010). These are catalogued separately under SECP's "Enforcement" section, organized by the underlying law.

## How to Access
All categories are searchable and browsable directly from SECP's main "Laws" navigation menu, with an "Advance Document Search" tool available for keyword/date-based searches across the full library of legal instruments.
