# SECP NBFC (Non-Banking Finance Company) Licensing

## Overview
Non-Banking Finance Companies (NBFCs) are entities licensed by SECP to carry out specified financial services other than deposit-taking banking. NBFCs are governed by **Part VIII-A of the Companies Ordinance, 1984** (as continued/read with the Companies Act, 2017 framework), the **Non-Banking Finance Companies (Establishment and Regulation) Rules, 2003**, and the **Non-Banking Finance Companies and Notified Entities Regulations, 2008**.

## Categories of NBFC Licenses
1. **Asset Management Services** (Asset Management Company / AMC — manages mutual funds)
2. **Investment Advisory Services**
3. **PE and VC Fund Management Services** (Private Fund Management Company / PFMC, under the Private Fund Regulations, 2015)
4. **Investment Finance Services**
5. **Leasing** (License to Undertake Leasing Business)
6. **Housing Finance Services**
7. **Voluntary Pension Funds** (Pension Fund Managers, under the Voluntary Pension System Rules, 2005)
8. **Modarabas** (regulated separately under the Modaraba (Floatation and Control) Ordinance, 1980, but supervised by SECP)
9. **REIT Management Services** (Real Estate Investment Trust Management Companies)
10. **Lending NBFCs**, including Non-Bank Microfinance Companies (NBMFCs) and digital lending apps (since the 2015 regulatory reclassification into "Lending NBFCs" and "Asset Management NBFCs")

## Eligibility / General Requirements
- Must be incorporated (or propose to be incorporated) as a **public limited company** under the Companies Act, 2017, read with the NBFC Rules, 2003.
- **Minimum equity** requirements vary significantly by license category — commonly cited ranges span roughly **PKR 50 million to PKR 1,000 million (PKR 1 billion)** depending on the type of NBFC (e.g., asset management companies have been cited around PKR 200 million, while investment finance companies have been cited around PKR 750 million; figures are periodically revised by SECP notification, so the current minimum equity for the specific license sought should always be confirmed with SECP's Licensing Department).
- **Promoters must be issued at least 25%** of the paid-up share capital of the NBFC.
- Promoters, directors, and the chief executive must satisfy SECP's **fit and proper criteria** (integrity, financial soundness, relevant experience/qualifications).
- No person may hold or become beneficial owner of **10% or more of the shares** of an NBFC without prior SECP approval; SECP can require divestment if a holder subsequently fails the fit-and-proper test.
- Foreign investors may establish NBFCs in Pakistan, subject to SECP approval and compliance with applicable foreign investment rules; foreign directors typically require security clearance.
- At least one director must have senior-management-level experience of at least five years in the financial sector.
- The NBFC's Chief Financial/Accounting Officer must be a chartered accountant, cost and management accountant, or hold a relevant master's degree with at least five years of senior financial-sector experience.

## Step-by-Step Process (Typical Sequence for Most NBFC Categories)
1. **Application for a No Objection Certificate (NOC)** — Submitted to the Licensing Department, SECP Headquarters, Islamabad, along with the category-specific checklist, to obtain permission to form an NBFC for the intended activity (e.g., Asset Management, PE/VC Fund Management, Investment Finance/Leasing/Housing Finance).
2. **NOC Granted** — SECP's Licensing/Specialized Companies Division issues an NOC for formation of the NBFC.
3. **Incorporation** — The NBFC is incorporated as a public limited company at the concerned Company Registration Office, following standard incorporation procedures, forms, and fees.
4. **License Application** — Submit the formal license application to SECP with evidence of paid-up capital, fit-and-proper documentation for sponsors/directors/CEO, business plan, and compliance undertakings.
5. **Grant of License** — On satisfaction of all requirements, SECP grants the operating license authorizing the NBFC to commence the specified financial service.

## Ongoing Regulatory Obligations
- Maintain accurate, up-to-date books of accounts in accordance with International Accounting Standards (IAS), audited by an SECP-licensed audit firm.
- Submit **quarterly (unaudited)** and **annual (audited)** financial statements to SECP.
- Obtain an **annual credit rating** from an SECP-registered credit rating agency and publish it in annual and quarterly reports.
- Report any significant change in ownership, management, or financial condition to SECP promptly.
- Comply with anti-money laundering/counter-terrorist-financing (AML/CFT) directions.
- Restrictions on removing company records/documents from Pakistan without SECP's prior permission.
- Disclose all credit facilities exceeding 20% of the NBFC's equity in its accounts.
- The chief executive of an NBFC cannot hold a similar position in any other company, except an investment company managed by the NBFC itself; any change of CEO or board composition requires SECP approval.

## Fees
NBFC license/NOC/incorporation fees follow SECP's standard fee schedule for company incorporation plus category-specific licensing fees set by the Licensing Department; these should be confirmed directly with SECP at the time of application, as they are periodically revised.
