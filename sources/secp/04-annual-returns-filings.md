# SECP Annual Returns and Company Filings

## Overview
Every company registered with SECP under the Companies Act, 2017 must submit an **annual return** and keep SECP updated on changes in directors/officers and financial statements. These are recurring statutory obligations that apply regardless of whether the company is actively trading — there is **no exemption for dormant or newly incorporated companies** once they exist on the register.

## Legal Basis
- **Section 130, Companies Act, 2017** — annual return.
- **Section 197, Companies Act, 2017** — register of officers / notification of changes (Form 29).
- **Section 437, Companies Act, 2017** — annual filing obligations of foreign companies (Form 45).

## Key Annual/Statutory Forms

### Form A / Form B — Annual Return
- A company **having share capital** files its annual return on **Form A**.
- A company **not having share capital** (e.g., a guarantee/Section 42 company) files on **Form B**.
- The annual return is a snapshot, as of the date of the AGM (or, if no AGM is held/concluded, the last day of the calendar year), giving details of: chief executive, directors, chief financial officer, company secretary, legal adviser, auditors, registered office address, members, and share capital structure.
- **Filing deadline:**
  - Listed companies: within **30 days** of holding the AGM, extendable up to **45 days** by the Registrar.
  - Other companies (unlisted/private, including SMCs which need not hold an AGM): within 30 days of the date the AGM was/would have been due.
- No supporting documents are typically required to be attached to Form A itself.
- Financial statements (balance sheet, profit & loss account, cash flow statement, statement of changes in equity, notes) are filed as a related but separate annual filing ("Accounts").

### Form 29 — Particulars of Directors, Officers, and Changes Therein
- Filed whenever there is an **appointment, cessation, or change in particulars** of a director, chief executive, CFO, company secretary, legal adviser, or auditor.
- Must be filed within **15 days** of the appointment/change (some guidance cites 10 days for officers to provide details internally, followed by 15 days for the company to file with SECP).
- Frequently filed together with Form A after an AGM where directors/auditors are re-elected or changed.
- A change of 25% or more in shareholding is generally expected to be reported promptly rather than held over to the next annual return; changes of less than 25% may be reported through the annual return.

### Form 45 — Foreign Companies
- Foreign companies with a place of business in Pakistan must file a list of Pakistani members/debenture holders and places of business, together with annual financial statements, under Section 437.

## Filing Channel
Annual returns and related forms are filed electronically through SECP's **eServices/eZfile** portal using the authorized signatory's login credentials and digital signature; physical/manual filing at the relevant Company Registration Office remains available as a fallback.

## Practical/Common Deadline Note
Multiple practitioner sources note that, in practice, many companies target **on or before October 28** each year as an informal "housekeeping" deadline for Form A/Form 29, though the legal deadline is tied to each company's own AGM date (30–45 days after the AGM), not a fixed calendar date for all companies.

## Penalties for Late/Non-Filing
- Failure to file can trigger a **show-cause notice** from the Registrar.
- If the company fails to respond satisfactorily, a **penalty of up to PKR 25,000** may be imposed.
- For continuing default, an additional **daily fine (commonly cited at around PKR 500/day)** may apply until the default is cured.
- A company in default on its filings cannot cleanly issue shares, transfer ownership, access certain banking facilities, or proceed to easy exit/winding up until its filings are brought current.

## Other "Running a Company" Filings
- **Accounts** — annual financial statements (audited, where applicable) filed under the Companies Act, 2017.
- **Changes in Company** — filings for change of registered office, change of company name, alteration of share capital, changes in memorandum/articles, etc., each with its own statutory form and fee.
