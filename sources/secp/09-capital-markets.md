# SECP Capital Markets Licensing

## Overview
SECP regulates Pakistan's capital markets infrastructure and intermediaries under the **Securities Act, 2015**, the **Securities Brokers (Licensing and Operations) Regulations, 2016**, the **Stock Exchanges (Corporatization, Demutualization and Integration) Act, 2012**, the **Central Depositories Act, 1997**, the **Futures Market Act, 2016**, and related rules/regulations. This covers the exchanges themselves, market infrastructure institutions, and the various classes of licensed intermediaries.

## Categories of Capital Market Licenses/Registrations
1. **Stock Exchanges** (currently the Pakistan Stock Exchange Limited, PSX)
2. **Commodity Exchanges** (e.g., Pakistan Mercantile Exchange, PMEX)
3. **Depository Companies** (Central Depository Company, CDC)
4. **Clearing Houses** (e.g., National Clearing Company of Pakistan Limited, NCCPL)
5. **Securities Brokers / Agents**
6. **Debt Securities Trustees (DST)**
7. **Share Registrars and Balloters (Transfer Agents)**
8. **Underwriters / Consultants / Bankers to the Issue**
9. **Credit Rating Companies**
10. **Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs)** — used for securitization/sukuk issuance
11. **Securities and Futures Advisors**

## Securities Brokers — Detailed Eligibility & Process
### Eligibility
- Must hold a **Trading Right Entitlement Certificate (TREC)** from the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) before applying to SECP for a broker's license (per Regulation 5 of the Securities Brokers (Licensing and Operations) Regulations, 2016).
- Must satisfy **Fit & Proper Criteria** for sponsors and management (directors, CEO) — covering integrity, solvency, and professional competence.
- Must maintain the prescribed **minimum capital / Net Capital Balance (NCB)** and **Base Minimum Capital (BMC)** requirements set by PSX/SECP, and undergo periodic NCB and system/balance audits.
- Must comply with client-asset segregation requirements and submit periodic Client Asset Segregation Statements.

### Process
1. Obtain a TREC from PSX.
2. Submit an application (Form A) to PSX, Admin Block, Stock Exchange Road, Karachi, along with fit-and-proper documentation, information about directors/sponsors, and required undertakings/affidavits.
3. PSX scrutinizes the application and forwards it to SECP for review and approval.
4. Upon SECP approval, the license is issued to PSX, which forwards it to the TREC holder/brokerage firm.
5. **License validity:** one (1) year, renewable annually with updated documentation.
6. **Application/issuance fee:** commonly cited at **PKR 100,000**, deposited at the time of submission (renewal fees apply separately; confirm current amounts with PSX/SECP).

### Ongoing Obligations for Brokers
- Confirmation of clients' orders and adherence to mandatory tariff structures.
- Fortnightly submission of Client Assets Segregation Statements to the exchange.
- Quarterly account statements to be shared with clients.
- Submission of customer grievance details as required under the 2016 Regulations.
- Reporting of trading by directors and substantial shareholders of the brokerage.
- Prior SECP approval required for any change of sponsor, CEO, or board composition.

## Other Intermediaries (Brief Eligibility Notes)
- **Debt Securities Trustees (DST):** licensed under the Debt Securities Trustee Regulations, 2012; must meet fit-and-proper and operational-capacity requirements to act as trustee for debt security issues (e.g., Term Finance Certificates/sukuk).
- **Share Registrars & Balloters:** license entities that maintain share registers and conduct balloting for public offers/IPOs; requires demonstrated technical/operational capacity and fit-and-proper management.
- **Underwriters/Consultants/Bankers to the Issue:** licensed to participate in public offerings (IPOs), subject to capital adequacy and professional-conduct requirements under the Public Offering Regulations, 2017.
- **Credit Rating Companies:** licensed under SECP's credit rating company regulations, requiring methodology approval, independence safeguards, and analyst competency standards.
- **Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs):** registered for specific securitization/structured-finance transactions under SECP's SPV rules.
- **Securities and Futures Advisors:** licensed to provide investment advice on securities/futures contracts, subject to fit-and-proper and (where applicable) professional certification requirements.

## Fees
Capital market license/registration fees vary by category and are prescribed in the relevant SECP regulation or notified separately (e.g., the PKR 100,000 broker license fee referenced above). Applicants should confirm current fees directly with SECP's Securities Market Division or PSX before applying.
