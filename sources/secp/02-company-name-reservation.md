# SECP Company Name Reservation

## Overview
Name reservation is the mandatory first step before incorporating a company or an LLP with SECP. Only a name that has been successfully reserved (and is still valid) allows an applicant to proceed to incorporation. The process is fully digitized via **eZfile**, though it can also be filed manually with a Company Registration Office (CRO).

## Eligibility / Who Can Apply
- Any promoter, subscriber, or authorized representative intending to incorporate a company or LLP.
- Applicants must first register as a user on SECP's eServices/eZfile system (CNIC/passport, mobile number, and email required) before submitting a name application.

## Naming Criteria (What Makes a Name Acceptable)
Under the Companies Act, 2017 and SECP's Guidelines for Company Name Reservation, a proposed name will generally be **rejected** if it:
- Is identical with, or closely resembles (including phonetically), the name of an existing company, LLP, registered trademark, or dissolved entity whose name is still protected.
- Is undesirable in the opinion of the Registrar.
- Is offensive to any section of society or designed to exploit religious sensitivities.
- Contains a **prohibited word** (see SECP's published "Prohibited Words" list) — this commonly includes words suggesting government affiliation or authority (e.g., Federal, State, Authority, Government, Pakistan used in a misleading manner, President, Governor, Prime Minister, Minister, Cabinet, Senate, National/Provincial Assembly, Parliament, Court, Judge, Regulator, Bank, Insurance, Modaraba, Cooperative, etc.) unless the relevant NOC/license or special permission has first been obtained from the concerned authority (e.g., State Bank of Pakistan for "Bank," SECP Insurance Division for "Insurance/Takaful").
- Implies patronage of, or connection with, a foreign government or an international body without authorization.
- Suggests a connection with a notable political personality without consent.

## Process
1. **User Registration** on eServices/eZfile (one-time; a small user registration fee applies).
2. **Name Search** — Use the free online Company Name Search tool to check availability before applying: https://eservices.secp.gov.pk/eServices/NameSearch.jsp
3. **Submit Application** — File the name reservation request (up to two alternative names may typically be proposed), either standalone or combined with the incorporation application, through eZfile.
4. **Pay the Prescribed Fee** via the eZfile-generated bank challan, credit/debit card, or 1-Bill; the challan must generally be paid within about 7 days of application or the request may be rejected/lapse.
5. **Scrutiny by Registrar** — The CRO checks the name against the SECP database and naming criteria above.
6. **Approval/Rejection** — If rejected, the applicant may submit a fresh name or file an appeal against the rejection through the mechanism described in SECP's Name Reservation Guidelines.
7. **Validity Period** — Once reserved, the name is held for the applicant for a limited period (commonly cited as up to 60 days) within which the incorporation application must be filed; otherwise the reservation lapses and the fee (non-refundable) must be paid again for a fresh request.

## Fees (indicative — always verify current Seventh Schedule rates)
- Name reservation fee: commonly cited at around **PKR 200–1,000** depending on the applicable fee-schedule update (SECP revised this fee effective November 8, 2024); older/offline filings may attract different (generally higher) rates than online filings.
- The fee is **non-refundable** even if the name is rejected.
- A separate nominal **user registration fee** (approx. PKR 100–200) applies once, at the time of first submission on eZfile/eServices.

## Special Cases
- **Sister concern/associated company:** if a similar name to an existing company is proposed for a group/sister concern, a board resolution signed by all directors of the existing company consenting to the similar name must generally be attached.
- **LLPs:** name reservation for an LLP follows a parallel process using **LLP Form I** (Application for Reservation of Name) rather than the standard company name-reservation form; the word "LLP" must be included in the reserved name.
- **Change/rectification of an already-reserved or registered name:** handled via a separate application (e.g., LLP Form II for LLPs; company name-change applications for companies), and attracts its own fee.
