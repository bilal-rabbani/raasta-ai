# SECP LLP (Limited Liability Partnership) Registration

## Overview
The Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) is a hybrid business vehicle introduced in Pakistan by SECP in 2017 to give entrepreneurs a partnership-style, flexible management structure while limiting each partner's personal liability — something a traditional partnership (Association of Persons/AOP) under the Partnership Act, 1932 does not offer. An LLP is a **separate legal entity** distinct from its partners, with perpetual succession, and is registered with and regulated by SECP.

## Governing Law
- **Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2017**
- **Limited Liability Partnership Regulations, 2018** (procedure for registration, naming, partners, conversion, etc.)

## Eligibility
- **Two (2) or more persons** associated for carrying on a lawful business with a view to profit may form an LLP.
- Partners can be individuals (Pakistani CNIC holders or foreign passport holders) or body corporates.
- At least the prescribed minimum number of partners must be designated as **"designated partners"** responsible for regulatory compliance.
- Existing partnerships (AOPs) or private companies may convert into an LLP subject to the conversion procedure and required consents.

## Key Features
- Separate legal entity; can own property, sue, and be sued in its own name.
- Partner liability limited to the amount of the partner's agreed contribution.
- No minimum capital requirement.
- Internal governance (profit sharing, management rights, admission/exit of partners) is set out in a private **LLP Agreement** rather than rigid statutory articles.
- Taxed similarly to an AOP — the LLP itself typically is not taxed separately; partners report their share of profit individually (subject to prevailing FBR/tax rules).

## Registration Process
1. **Name Selection & Reservation** — Choose a unique name (must include "LLP") that does not resemble an existing company or LLP name and avoids prohibited words listed in Section 6 of the LLP Act (e.g., Federal/Provincial Government, Authority, Commission, Registrar, Cooperative, Bureau, Municipal, Union, Republic, President/Governor/Prime Minister/Minister, Cabinet, Senate, National/Provincial Assembly, Parliament, Statutory, Court/Judiciary/Judge, Jury, Administrator). File **LLP Form I** (Application for Reservation of Name).
2. **Draft the LLP Agreement** — Defines rights, duties, contribution, and profit-sharing of partners.
3. **File Incorporation Application** — Submit **LLP Form III** (Application for Incorporation of LLP) along with the LLP Agreement and partner consent forms (**LLP Form IV** — Consent to act as Partner/Designated Partner).
4. **Partner Documentation** — CNICs for Pakistani partners; notarized/attested passport copies for foreign partners.
5. **Pay the Prescribed SECP Fee** — Via the eServices/eZfile portal (online) or bank challan (offline, attached to the physical application).
6. **SECP Review** — SECP scrutinizes the application for compliance with the LLP Act, 2017 and LLP Regulations, 2018.
7. **Certificate of Incorporation** — Issued upon approval, confirming the LLP's legal existence.
8. **Post-Registration Compliance** — Obtain an NTN from FBR, open a bank account in the LLP's name, and (if hiring staff) register with EOBI/provincial social security; register for sales tax if applicable.

## Other Key LLP Forms (SECP)
- **LLP Form II** — Application for change or rectification of name
- **LLP Form V** — Notice of induction and cessation of partners
- **LLP Form VI** — Application for conversion of a firm or company into an LLP
- **LLP Form VII** — Change of registered address
- **LLP Form VIII** — Change of LLP Agreement
- **LLP Form IX** — Notice to partners for providing particulars of ultimate beneficial owners
- **LLP Form X** — Declaration by partner about ultimate beneficial owners
- **LLP Form XI** — Declaration by partner about change of ultimate beneficial owners
- **LLP Form XII** — Declaration of compliance with Section 8 of the LLP Act, 2017

## Processing Time
Once documentation is complete and fees are paid, SECP typically registers an LLP within **one to two weeks**, though this can vary based on completeness of the application and any name-related objections.

## Fees
Fees are prescribed by SECP and vary with the amount of partners' contribution; both online (eServices/eZfile) and offline (bank challan) payment channels are available. Because fee tables are amended periodically by SECP notification, applicants should confirm current amounts via the SECP fee schedule before filing (see the Fees file in this set).

## Why Choose an LLP over a Private Limited Company or AOP
- Cheaper to form/maintain than a private limited company but offers stronger liability protection than an AOP.
- Suitable for professional service firms, small partnerships, and businesses that do not need to raise capital by issuing shares.
- Lighter ongoing compliance burden than a company, though partners must still meet LLP Act filing obligations (e.g., notifying changes in partners, registered address, or the LLP Agreement).
