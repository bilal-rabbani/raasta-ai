# SECP Fees and Fee Calculators

## Overview
All fees payable to SECP for company/LLP registration, filings, and licensing are prescribed under the **Seventh Schedule to the Companies Act, 2017**, which SECP is empowered to amend from time to time by notification (S.R.O.). SECP has periodically revised these fees — including reductions in past years to promote formalization, and subsequent increases (e.g., **Circular No. 13 of 2024** and a revised fee structure effective **April 21, 2025**) — so applicants should always verify the current schedule before budgeting or paying.

## Key Principle: Online (Electronic) Filing Is Cheaper Than Manual Filing
SECP's fee structure consistently charges a **lower fee for electronic submission via eZfile/eServices** than for manual/physical submission of the same document — in some cases, the manual fee is double or more than the electronic fee. Where no electronic fee has been prescribed for a document, it can only be filed physically.

## Illustrative Fee Categories (Always Confirm Current Rates)

### User Registration
- One-time eServices/eZfile user registration fee: approx. **PKR 100–200** per user.

### Name Reservation
- Name reservation fee: revised effective November 8, 2024; commonly cited in the range of **PKR 200–1,000** depending on the source/date consulted. Non-refundable even if the name is rejected.

### Company Incorporation (Based on Authorized Share Capital)
- Fee is tiered/slabbed according to the company's authorized share capital — the more capital authorized, the higher the incorporation fee.
- For companies with a share capital, a base fee applies to the first slab (e.g., capital up to PKR 100,000), with additional fee increments per further PKR 100,000 (or similar slab) of authorized capital.
- Per SECP's April 21, 2025 fee structure, a company having share capital may pay a base fee (cited around **PKR 6,050** in one reported table) for a starting nominal share capital slab — figures vary by company type and by whether the company is limited by shares or by guarantee (companies limited by guarantee/no share capital, other than Section 42 companies, may face a different fee, e.g., a reported **PKR 20,000 electronic / PKR 33,000 manual** fee for registering/converting into a guarantee company).
- **Single Member Company (SMC):** a flat non-refundable licensing-related fee has been cited around **PKR 25,000** in some fee guides (confirm against the current Seventh Schedule, as SMC fee treatment has also been revised over time).
- SECP had earlier reduced the first-slab capital fee for new incorporations to **PKR 1,000 (online) / PKR 2,000 (offline)**, from previous levels of PKR 2,500/5,000, as part of a fee-rationalization exercise; subsequent notifications (2024/2025) have since revised rates upward again.

### Ancillary Incorporation-Related Fees
- Certificate of Incorporation (certified copy): approx. **PKR 100 (online) / PKR 600 (offline)**.
- Memorandum and Articles of Association filing: approx. **PKR 250**.

### Annual Return / Statutory Filing Fees
- Filing fees for Form A/B and other statutory forms are also capital-based/slabbed, with a reduced online rate; SECP has cited filing fees starting as low as **PKR 250 (online)** for small entities, with higher rates for manual filing.
- Late filing can attract penalties in addition to the base filing fee (see the Annual Returns file for penalty specifics — commonly up to PKR 25,000 plus a daily fine for continuing default).

### LLP Fees
- LLP name reservation, incorporation, and subsequent filings (change of partners, registered address, LLP agreement, etc.) each carry their own prescribed fee under the LLP Regulations, 2018, generally following the same online/offline fee-differential principle as companies.

### Licensing Fees (Sector-Specific)
- Insurance, NBFC, and capital-market intermediary licenses carry their own fee schedules set by the relevant SECP division (e.g., a securities broker license application fee has been cited around **PKR 100,000**, deposited at the time of submission to PSX/SECP).

### Authorized Share Capital Enhancement Fee
- Where a company later increases its authorized share capital, an additional fee is payable, calculated using SECP's **Authorized Share Capital Fee Calculator**, based on the incremental capital being authorized.

## Online Tools Provided by SECP
- **Company Incorporation Fee Calculator** — estimates the incorporation fee based on company type and proposed authorized capital.
- **Authorized Share Capital Fee Calculator** — estimates the fee for enhancing an existing company's authorized share capital.
- **Schedule of Fee / Seventh Schedule** — the authoritative, downloadable fee table referenced by all SECP fee calculations.

## How to Pay
- **Online:** via eZfile-generated challan, paid by debit/credit card, 1-Bill, or online banking.
- **Offline/Manual:** via a bank challan deposited at a designated bank branch, with the paid challan then attached to the physical application submitted at a CRO.
