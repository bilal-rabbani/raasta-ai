# Auctions & Tenders — FBR

## 1. Auctions (Customs Auctions)

**Overview:** Pakistan Customs (under FBR) periodically auctions goods that are confiscated, abandoned, or uncleared beyond the permissible warehousing period.

**Eligibility**
- Open to any member of the public/business wishing to bid, subject to registration with the conducting Collectorate and any bidder eligibility criteria specified in the auction notice

**Requirements**
- CNIC/NTN
- Earnest money / bid security as specified in the individual auction notice
- Registration with the relevant Customs Collectorate conducting the auction, where required

**Process**
1. Check the "Auctions" section on fbr.gov.pk (or the relevant Collectorate's notices) for upcoming auction announcements.
2. Review the auction catalogue/notice for item details, location, and terms.
3. Submit bid security/earnest money as specified.
4. Participate in the auction (physical or online, per the notice) on the scheduled date.
5. Successful bidders complete payment and clearance formalities per the auction terms.

**Fees**
- Bid/earnest money as specified per auction; final cost is the winning bid amount plus any applicable duties/taxes not already covered.

## 2. Tenders

**Overview:** FBR publishes tenders for procurement of goods, services, and works needed for its own operations (IT systems, office supplies, construction, etc.).

**Eligibility**
- Open to vendors/contractors meeting the qualification criteria stated in each tender document (registration, financial capacity, technical experience, etc.)

**Requirements**
- Company/vendor registration documents (NTN, sales tax registration if applicable)
- Tender-specific documents (technical proposal, financial bid, bid security/earnest money) as specified in the Notice Inviting Tender (NIT)

**Process**
1. Check the "Tenders" section on fbr.gov.pk for current notices (also typically published in national newspapers and PPRA's website per Public Procurement Regulatory Authority rules).
2. Purchase/download the tender document as instructed in the notice.
3. Prepare and submit technical and financial bids by the stated deadline, along with any required bid security.
4. Bids are opened and evaluated per the criteria in the tender document; the contract is awarded to the successful bidder.

**Fees**
- Tender document fee (if any) and bid security amount are specified in each individual notice; these vary by procurement.
