# Acts, Ordinances, Rules, SROs & Notifications — FBR

**Overview:** This is a reference/repository service rather than an application-based service — it provides public access to Pakistan's federal tax legal framework.

**Eligibility**
- Open to the general public, taxpayers, tax practitioners, and researchers — no registration required

**What's Covered**
- **Acts:** e.g., Sales Tax Act 1990, Federal Excise Act 2005
- **Ordinances:** e.g., Income Tax Ordinance, 2001
- **Rules:** e.g., Income Tax Rules 2001, Sales Tax Rules 2006
- **SROs (Statutory Regulatory Orders) / Notifications:** amendments, rate changes, and exemptions notified periodically (e.g., valuation SROs, sales tax rate SROs)
- **Orders / Circulars:** clarificatory circulars explaining amendments (e.g., annual circulars explaining Finance Act changes to the Income Tax Ordinance, Sales Tax Act, and Federal Excise Act)

**How to Access**
1. Visit fbr.gov.pk and go to the relevant menu item (Acts / Ordinance / Rules / SROs-Notifications / Orders-Circulars).
2. Browse or search by year, tax type, or keyword.
3. Documents are generally downloadable as PDFs.

**Requirements**
- None — publicly accessible

**Fees**
- Free of charge

**Notes**
- Because SROs and circulars are issued frequently (including after each Finance Act), it's advisable to check the FBR site directly for the latest version rather than relying on older summaries when determining current rates or exemptions.
