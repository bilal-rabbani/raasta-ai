# Sales Tax & Federal Excise Duty (FED) — FBR Services

## 1. Sales Tax Registration

**Overview:** Governed by the Sales Tax Act, 1990. Required for persons/businesses making taxable supplies of goods or specified services in Pakistan.

**Eligibility (who must register)**
- Manufacturers (regardless of turnover)
- Importers
- Wholesalers, distributors, and dealers
- Retailers with turnover exceeding the exemption threshold
- Exporters (registration needed to claim zero-rating/refunds even though exports are largely exempt)
- Service providers required under Provincial/Federal law to register (hotels, clubs, caterers, customs agents, courier services, stevedores, ship chandlers, etc.)
- Persons required to be registered who avoid doing so may be compulsorily registered by FBR after inquiry (Rule 6, Sales Tax Rules 2006)

**Registration Thresholds / Exemptions**
- Manufacturers with taxable turnover below the prescribed threshold (approx. Rs. 5 million, subject to periodic revision) and utility bills below the prescribed limit in the last 12 months are exempt from registration.
- Retailers with total turnover below the prescribed limit (approx. Rs. 5 million in the last 12 months) are similarly exempt.

**Requirements / Documents**
- CNIC/NTN of the applicant/business
- Business incorporation/registration documents (SECP certificate, partnership deed, etc., as applicable)
- Bank account details
- Proof of business premises (utility bill, tenancy agreement, or ownership document)
- Completed Sales Tax Registration Form (STR-1)

**Application Process**
1. Apply online through the FBR e-portal (IRIS) or complete Form STR-1.
2. If applying manually, submit the form and documents at the nearest FBR Regional Tax Office (RTO)/Sales Tax Collectorate.
3. FBR/PRAL verifies the submitted information.
4. On successful verification, a Sales Tax Registration Number (STRN) and Sales Tax Registration Certificate are issued.

**Fees / Processing Time**
- No government fee for registration itself.
- Processing typically takes about 7–10 working days for manual applications; online IRIS registration is generally faster.

**Ongoing Compliance**
- Registered persons must file monthly sales tax returns declaring output tax, input tax, and net payable/refundable amount.
- Standard sales tax rate: 18% of value of supplies on most goods (higher rates of 18.5%–21% apply to specific items per relevant SROs).

## 2. Federal Excise Duty (FED) Registration

**Overview:** FED applies to specified excisable goods and services (e.g., cigarettes, beverages, cement, certain services) under the Federal Excise Act, 2005.

**Eligibility**
- Businesses manufacturing, producing, or providing services on the excisable goods/services list
- Businesses crossing FBR's registration threshold for excisable activity

**Requirements**
- Same core documents as sales tax registration (CNIC/NTN, business registration proof, bank details, premises proof)

**Application Process**
1. Register through FBR's online portal (IRIS), similar to the sales tax registration workflow.
2. Maintain records of excisable production, sales, or service delivery.
3. File periodic (usually monthly) FED returns and remit duty collected.

**Fees / Processing Time**
- No separate registration fee; duty itself is remitted per return based on production/sales value.
- Exported excisable goods are generally exempt from FED, similar to sales tax zero-rating, subject to current notifications.

**Notes**
- Many excisable goods are also subject to sales tax simultaneously — businesses should review both obligations together.
