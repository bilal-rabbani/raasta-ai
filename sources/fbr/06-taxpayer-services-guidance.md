# Taxpayer Services & Guidance — FBR

**Overview:** FBR provides a range of facilitation channels to help taxpayers understand obligations and resolve routine queries, distinct from formal legal dispute resolution (covered separately).

**Eligibility**
- Open to all registered and prospective taxpayers, including individuals, businesses, and withholding agents

**Available Channels / Services**
- **Publications & Brochures:** FATE Wing (Facilitation and Taxpayer Education) publishes taxpayer facilitation guides (e.g., on ADR, registration, filing) available for download on the FBR website.
- **Taxpayers Audit information:** guidance on how the audit selection and process works.
- **Tax Asaan App:** mobile app for NTN/ATL verification, e-payments, and simplified sales tax registration for businesses.
- **Helpline/Feedback:** a "Feedback" channel is listed on the FBR portal for general queries and service feedback.
- **Seminars/Workshops:** FBR periodically holds sessions (e.g., "Asaan Tajir Return filing" seminars) with chambers of commerce and trade bodies to guide specific taxpayer segments (traders, retailers, etc.).

**How to Access**
1. Visit fbr.gov.pk and check the "Publications / Brochures" and "Press Releases / Events" sections for current guidance material and scheduled seminars.
2. Download the Tax Asaan App (Android/iOS) for on-the-go verification and payment services.
3. Use the "Feedback" link on the FBR site for general questions or to report a service issue (distinct from formal complaints — see the Complaints & Dispute Resolution file for that process).

**Requirements**
- None to access general publications; app-based services require an NTN/CNIC as with other online services.

**Fees**
- All taxpayer education and guidance material is free.
