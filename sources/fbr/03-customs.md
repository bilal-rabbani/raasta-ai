# Customs — FBR Services

## 1. Customs Registration (WeBOC)

**Overview:** To use Pakistan's online customs clearance portal, WeBOC, traders/agents must first register with FBR.

**Eligibility**
- Importers, exporters, clearing agents, and other persons conducting customs-related transactions

**Requirements**
- Completed WeBOC external user registration form
- NTN and CNIC/company registration documents
- Bank account details

**Application Process**
1. Download the external user registration form from the FBR Customs portal.
2. Complete and submit it along with supporting documents to the relevant Collectorate.
3. Once verified, credentials for the WeBOC system are issued.

**Fees / Processing Time**
- No specific registration fee is published; processing depends on document verification by the Collectorate.

## 2. Customs Tariff & Duty Calculation

**Overview:** The Customs Tariff (Pakistan Customs Tariff / HS Code schedule) determines applicable duty rates for imported/exported goods based on HS classification.

**Eligibility**
- Any importer/exporter or member of the public needing to determine applicable duty on a good

**Requirements**
- Correct HS Code classification of the goods (an HS Transposition Table is available on the FBR site to map old/new codes)

**Process**
1. Identify the HS Code of the good using the Customs Tariff schedule or HS Transposition Table.
2. Use the online Duty Calculator (available on the FBR Customs portal) to estimate applicable duties/taxes.
3. Confirm classification/valuation disputes through Customs Valuations rulings if needed.

**Fees**
- No fee to look up tariff rates or use the duty calculator; actual duty is payable based on the calculated value.

---

## 3. Paying Customs Duty

**Overview:** Customs duty is paid through the WeBOC system.

**Process**
1. Log in to WeBOC.
2. Follow the WeBOC user guide/manual for payment creation (available on the FBR site).
3. Generate the relevant payment slip and pay through the designated banking channel.

**Fees / Processing Time**
- The duty amount is calculated per the Goods Declaration; payment posts once the bank confirms transfer.

## 4. Vehicle Import

**Overview:** Governs import of new/used vehicles into Pakistan, including special schemes.

**Eligibility / Schemes**
- General import: any person may import a new vehicle against payment of applicable duty and taxes under the general Import Policy Order.
- Transfer of Residence (TR), Personal Baggage, and Gift schemes: available to eligible overseas Pakistanis/returning residents per specific conditions.
- Duty-free import of a car (engine capacity up to 1350cc, new condition) for disabled persons, subject to recommendation by the Federal Board (Council) for Disabled Persons.

**Requirements**
- Import documentation (Bill of Lading, invoice, etc.)
- For TR/Baggage/Gift schemes: proof of eligibility (residence abroad duration, passport, NICOP, etc., per the specific scheme's procedure document)
- For disabled-persons scheme: certification/recommendation from the Federal Board for Disabled Persons

**Application Process**
1. Determine the applicable scheme (general import vs. TR/Baggage/Gift vs. disabled-persons concession).
2. Prepare and submit the Goods Declaration via WeBOC along with supporting documents.
3. Duty and taxes are assessed per the applicable structure; pay via WeBOC.
4. Clearance is granted after payment and verification.

**Fees / Processing Time**
- Duty/tax amount varies by vehicle type, engine capacity, and scheme; disabled-persons scheme and TR/Baggage/Gift schemes may reduce or waive duty subject to conditions.
- Refer to FBR's "Procedure for Import of Vehicles under various schemes" document for scheme-specific processing steps.

## 5. Travel Guide (Passenger/Baggage Rules)

**Overview:** Covers duty-free allowances and procedures for travelers entering/leaving Pakistan.

**Eligibility**
- All international travelers (Pakistani nationals and foreign visitors), with certain allowances specific to Pakistani nationals

**Key Topics Covered**
- Green Channel / Red Channel selection at airports based on whether goods need declaration
- Currency Declaration System (CDS) for carrying currency across the border
- Duty-free allowance under the Baggage Rules
- Transfer of Residence provisions for returning Pakistani nationals
- Regulations on prohibited/restricted articles and re-import of goods

**Process**
1. On arrival, choose Green Channel (nothing to declare, within allowance) or Red Channel (goods to declare/above allowance).
2. Declare currency above the prescribed threshold under CDS.
3. For goods exceeding the free allowance, duty is assessed and payable at the point of entry.

**Fees**
- Duty-free up to the prescribed baggage allowance; excess goods are dutiable per the applicable tariff.

## 6. Mobile Device Regularization (DIRBS)

**Overview:** DIRBS (Device Identification, Registration and Blocking System) is a joint FBR–PTA system for registering mobile phones brought into Pakistan, ensuring only compliant/type-approved devices function on local networks.

**Eligibility**
- International passengers bringing in mobile device(s) as accompanied baggage
- Commercial importers of mobile devices
- Local residents needing to register a device already in Pakistan

**Requirements**
- Passport/CNIC and travel details (for passengers)
- Device IMEI number(s)
- For commercial importers: PTA type-approval, list of IMEI numbers in CSV format, and Certificate of Clearance (COC) application through PTA's DIRBS portal (per Customs General Order 01 of 2024)

**Application Process (Passengers)**
1. Register the device via the DIRBS web-based interface within 60 days of arrival in Pakistan.
2. The system auto-validates details through WeBOC.
3. The first mobile phone registered in a year is allowed duty/tax-free under Baggage Rules.
4. For a second or additional device, the system generates a Payment Slip ID (PSID) for the applicable duty/taxes.
5. Pay via online banking, ATM, mobile banking, or bank branch.
6. Once payment is confirmed, the device is "white-listed" (registered) in DIRBS.

**Application Process (Commercial Importers)**
1. Apply for a Certificate of Clearance (COC) via PTA's DIRBS portal for devices with existing PTA type-approval.
2. Upload required documents, including IMEI numbers in CSV format.
3. Send an email to commercialnoc@pta.gov.pk for account activation.
4. Once activated, log in and apply for the COC.
5. DIRBS verifies IMEI numbers (checking against stolen/duplicate/cloned lists) and forwards the application to FBR's WeBOC system for customs processing.

**Fees / Processing Time**
- First phone/year: free of duty and tax under Baggage Rules allowance.
- Additional devices: duty/tax as calculated by the system, payable via generated PSID.
- Devices not registered within 60 days of arrival are registered later only on payment of duty/tax plus a prescribed fine, and lose the free-allowance benefit.
