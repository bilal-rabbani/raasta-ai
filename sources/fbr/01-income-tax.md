# Income Tax — FBR Services

## 1. NTN / Income Tax Registration

**Overview:** Registration with FBR creates a taxpayer profile on the IRIS portal. For individuals, the 13-digit CNIC itself functions as the NTN once registered; companies, AOPs, and foreign nationals receive a separate 7-digit NTN.

**Eligibility**
- Any individual earning taxable income (salaried, business, freelance, rental, etc.)
- Anyone required to file per Section 114(1) of the Income Tax Ordinance, 2001 — including property buyers/sellers, importers/exporters, and sellers of digitally ordered goods/services (Section 181)
- Companies, AOPs, and foreign nationals earning income in Pakistan

**Requirements / Documents**
- Valid CNIC (Pakistani individuals) or NICOP/Passport (overseas Pakistanis/foreign nationals)
- Personal mobile number (biometrically verified) and a personal email address
- For businesses: proof of business address, recent utility bill, bank account details, incorporation/registration certificate (SECP) where applicable, CNICs of partners/directors
- Form 181 (Registration Form) for businesses

**Application Process**
1. Create an account on the IRIS e-enrollment portal (iris.fbr.gov.pk) using CNIC/NICOP.
2. Complete the registration form (Form 181 for businesses) with personal/business details, address, and bank information.
3. Submit; the system verifies CNIC details against NADRA records.
4. On verification, NTN/registration is confirmed and a taxpayer profile is created on IRIS.
5. Companies/AOPs unable to complete registration online may need to visit the relevant Regional Tax Office (RTO).

**Fees / Processing Time**
- FBR charges no fee for registration.
- Individual registration is typically near-instant once CNIC verification passes; business registration may take longer if documents need manual review.

## 2. Filing Income Tax Returns

**Overview:** Every registered person (individual, AOP, or company) must file an annual income tax return declaring income, expenses, assets, and tax liability for the relevant tax year.

**Eligibility / Who Must File**
- Salaried individuals and other individuals with taxable income
- Associations of Persons (AOPs)
- Companies
- Anyone holding an NTN or who has received an FBR filing notice (a "nil return" is required even with zero income, to preserve Active Taxpayer status)

**Requirements**
- IRIS login credentials
- Salary certificate / income details, bank statements, wealth statement details (assets/liabilities)
- Withholding tax certificates, if applicable

**Application (Filing) Process**
1. Log in to IRIS (iris.fbr.gov.pk).
2. Navigate to Declaration → Income Tax Return → select the relevant Tax Year.
3. Enter income details, deductions, tax credits, and the wealth statement.
4. Verify computed tax liability; pay any balance due via a generated PSID (see Online Services file).
5. Submit the return electronically.

**Deadlines (Tax Year 2026, covering income from 1 July 2025 – 30 June 2026)**
- Salaried individuals & AOPs: 30 September 2026
- Companies (30 June year-end): 31 December 2026
- FBR has occasionally granted short extensions via SRO in past years, but these should not be assumed.

**Fees / Penalties**
- No fee to file.
- Late filing penalty: higher of 0.1% of tax payable per day or Rs. 1,000/day (subject to statutory minimums, commonly around Rs. 10,000 for individuals), capped at 50% of tax payable, under Section 182.
- A separate default surcharge applies under Section 205 on unpaid tax.
- Missing the deadline removes the taxpayer from the Active Taxpayer List (ATL) until the return is filed and, in some cases, an ATL surcharge is paid.

## 3. Active Taxpayer List (ATL)

**Overview:** The ATL is FBR's public, weekly-updated record of taxpayers who have filed their return for the relevant tax year. Being "active" (a filer) means lower withholding tax rates on banking, property, and vehicle transactions.

**Eligibility**
- Automatically included once the relevant year's income tax return is filed and any dues/surcharge are cleared.

**Requirements**
- CNIC (individuals) or NTN (companies/AOPs) to check status.

**Process to Check Status**
1. Online: FBR website → Online Services → Online Verification Portal → Active Taxpayer List (Income Tax) → enter CNIC/NTN and verification code.
2. SMS: Send "ATL" (space) 13-digit CNIC (individuals) or "ATL" (space) 7-digit NTN (companies/AOPs) to **9966**.
3. AJK Active Taxpayer status: send "AJKATL" (space) CNIC to 9966.
4. Via IRIS: check "Taxpayer Status" in your profile after login.

**Fees / Processing Time**
- Checking status is free.
- ATL is refreshed weekly (typically every Sunday).
- If inactive due to late filing, an ATL surcharge applies: Rs. 1,000 (individuals), Rs. 10,000 (AOPs), Rs. 20,000 (companies) to be restored, in addition to filing the pending return.

## 4. Withholding Tax (WHT) Compliance

**Overview:** Employers and businesses making specified payments (salary, services, rent, contracts, etc.) must deduct tax at source and deposit it with FBR monthly.

**Eligibility**
- Any withholding agent (employer, company, or business) required under sections such as 149 (salary), 153 (services/contracts), and 155 (rent) of the Income Tax Ordinance.

**Requirements**
- Business NTN and IRIS login
- Records of payments made and tax deducted per employee/vendor for the period

**Application / Deposit Process**
1. Log in to IRIS under the business NTN.
2. Go to Payments → Create Payment and select the relevant WHT section/head (e.g., 149 for salary, 153 for services, 155 for rent).
3. Select the applicable tax period (month) and enter the total WHT amount.
4. Generate a PSID and pay through a bank/online channel.
5. File the monthly WHT statement on IRIS under Section 165, generally due by the 15th of the following month (salary WHT deposits are typically due by the 7th).

**Fees / Processing Time**
- No fee for filing; only the tax amount deducted is payable.
- Payments post to IRIS automatically, usually within hours of bank confirmation.
