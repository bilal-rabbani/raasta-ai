# Budget Information — FBR

**Overview:** FBR plays a central role in preparing tax-related proposals for Pakistan's annual Federal Budget and in publishing the resulting Finance Act amendments. This is an informational resource rather than a citizen-facing application service.

**Eligibility**
- Open to the public — no registration needed to view budget documents and explanatory circulars

**What's Covered**
- **Budget proposals process:** FBR field formations and Chief Collectors/Directors General submit proposed tax law amendments (income tax, sales tax, customs, FED) as part of preparing each year's Finance Bill. A Tax Policy Office within the Ministry of Finance now coordinates this process.
- **Revenue targets:** FBR publishes its annual tax collection target (e.g., a Rs. 15.26 trillion target was set for FY2026-27) and periodic revenue performance updates.
- **Finance Act & explanatory circulars:** once the budget is passed, FBR issues circulars explaining amendments made to the Income Tax Ordinance 2001, Sales Tax Act 1990, and Federal Excise Act 2005 (e.g., "Circular No. 02 of 2026-27... Explanation of Important Amendments").
- **Budget document archive:** past years' Finance Bills/Acts and budget-in-brief documents.

**How to Access**
1. Visit fbr.gov.pk and look for the "Budget [Year]" link (e.g., "Budget 2026-27") on the homepage or under Publications.
2. Download the Finance Bill/Finance Act and the accompanying explanatory circulars for the relevant year.
3. Check "Press Releases" for revenue target announcements and budget-related updates throughout the year.

**Requirements**
- None — publicly accessible documents

**Fees**
- Free

**Notes**
- Because budget measures (rates, thresholds, exemptions) change every fiscal year via the Finance Act, always confirm the applicable year's circular before relying on a specific rate or threshold.
