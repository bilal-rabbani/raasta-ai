# Tax Directory — FBR

**Overview:** The Tax Directory is FBR's published record listing registered taxpayers along with basic information such as their declared tax payments for a given tax year, intended to promote transparency. It has historically been published annually for parliamentarians and, in various years, for the general taxpaying public.

**Eligibility**
- Open to public viewing on the FBR portal — no login required for search/lookup

**What It Shows (typically)**
- Taxpayer name
- NTN/CNIC (masked/partial, depending on the version published)
- City/RTO
- Tax paid for the relevant tax year

**How to Access**
1. Visit fbr.gov.pk and locate the "Tax Directory" link (listed under the main services/menu section of the site).
2. Search by name, CNIC, or NTN, depending on the search fields available for that year's directory.

**Requirements**
- None for searching; the directory is a published dataset rather than an application-based service.

**Fees**
- Free to search/view.

**Notes**
- Availability and searchable fields can vary by year depending on FBR's publication policy for that period; check the current directory page on fbr.gov.pk for the latest published edition.
