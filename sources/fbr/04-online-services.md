# Online Services — FBR

## 1. e-Payment (ePayment 2.0 / PSID System)

**Overview:** FBR's unified online tax payment system, now integrated within the IRIS 2.0 portal, covers Income Tax, Sales Tax, Federal Excise Duty, and Withholding Tax payments.

**Eligibility**
- Any registered or unregistered taxpayer needing to pay a federal tax, duty, or fine to FBR

**Requirements**
- NTN/CNIC (or FTN for entities)
- IRIS login (registered taxpayers) — unregistered persons can also generate certain PSIDs
- Active bank account or access to internet/mobile banking or ATM

**Process**
1. Log in to IRIS 2.0 and navigate to the e-Payment / Payments section.
2. Select the relevant tax type (Income Tax, Sales Tax, FED, Withholding Tax) and the specific payment head/section.
3. Enter the tax year/period and the amount payable.
4. Confirm the details to generate a unique 17-digit Payment Slip ID (PSID).
5. Pay via internet banking, ATM, mobile banking, or a bank branch (National Bank of Pakistan/State Bank-authorized banks), using the PSID as reference.
6. On confirmation, a Computerized Payment Receipt (CPR) is issued via email/SMS and becomes accessible within IRIS for record-keeping.

**Fees / Processing Time**
- No service fee for generating a PSID; only the tax/duty amount itself is payable.
- Payments are typically credited to the taxpayer's IRIS account within hours of bank confirmation, routed through the 1-Link interbank settlement network.
- PSIDs have a limited validity period, after which they may need to be regenerated if unpaid.

## 2. Online Verification Services (Taxpayer Profile Inquiry / ATL / NTN Verification)

**Overview:** A public-facing portal (and the Tax Asaan mobile app) allowing anyone to verify NTN registration, ATL/filer status, exemption certificates, and CPR utilization status.

**Eligibility**
- Open to the public — no login required for basic verification

**Requirements**
- CNIC (13 digits, no dashes) for individuals, or NTN (7 digits) for companies/AOPs

**Process**
1. Visit the FBR website → Online Services → Online Verification Portal.
2. Choose the desired check: "Taxpayer Profile Inquiry" (registration details), "Active Taxpayer List (Income Tax)," or "Active Taxpayer List (Sales Tax)."
3. Select the parameter type (CNIC or NTN), enter the number and the displayed verification/captcha code.
4. Click "Verify" to view the result (registration status, ATL status, registration date, RTO, etc.).
5. Alternatively, use the Tax Asaan mobile app for the same checks, plus Exemption Certificate verification and CPR Utilization Status.
6. For a quick check without internet: send an SMS to **9966** — format "ATL (space) CNIC" for individuals or "ATL (space) 7-digit NTN" for companies/AOPs.

**Fees / Processing Time**
- Free; results are returned instantly (online) or via immediate SMS reply.
- The Active Taxpayer List itself is refreshed weekly (typically every Sunday).

## 3. Customs Online Services (WeBOC-based)

**Overview:** A set of online tools for customs-related processes, additional to WeBOC registration and duty payment covered in the Customs file.

**Included Services**
- Duty Calculator
- Exchange Rate lookup
- HS Transposition Table
- Customs Valuation rulings and Valuation Meeting notices
- Export Facilitation Schemes and Customs Duty Drawback claims
- Currency Declaration System (CDS)

**Process**
- Each tool is accessed directly through the relevant section of the FBR Customs portal (fbr.gov.pk under "Customs" → "Online Services"); most require no login for informational lookups (e.g., duty calculator, exchange rates), while transactional actions (drawback claims, valuation submissions) are conducted through WeBOC using registered credentials.

**Fees**
- Informational tools (duty calculator, exchange rates, tariff lookup) are free to use.
