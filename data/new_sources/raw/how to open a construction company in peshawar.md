2\. Choose the legal structure
==============================

You can operate as:

### Option A — Sole proprietorship

Simpler for a small operation.

### Option B — Partnership/AOP

Useful if there are multiple owners.

### Option C — Private Limited Company

Usually more appropriate for a substantial factory because ownership, investment and liability are structured.

For a company, registration is through Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP).

SECP's current process provides online company name reservation and incorporation through eZfile.

### Basic company documents

Typically prepare:

*   Proposed company name
    
*   CNICs of directors/shareholders
    
*   Registered office address
    
*   Principal business activity
    
*   Memorandum of Association
    
*   Articles of Association
    
*   Shareholding details
    
*   Contact information
    
*   Capital/share structure

5\. Apply for an industrial plot
================================

For a KPEZDMC project, the general process is:

1.  Prepare feasibility report.
    
2.  Prepare project/business plan.
    
3.  Identify required industrial sector.
    
4.  Determine required plot size.
    
5.  Submit plot application.
    
6.  KPEZDMC evaluates the project.
    
7.  Receive acceptance/allotment.
    
8.  Complete payment according to applicable terms.
    
9.  Site visit/demarcation.
    
10.  Receive provisional/final allotment documentation.
    
11.  Submit building drawings.
    
12.  Construct the factory.
    
13.  Install machinery.
    
14.  Obtain required operational approvals.
    
15.  Commence production.
    

KP-BOIT's published procedure also specifies building-plan and construction/production milestones for KPEZDMC plots.

You may need approval/clearance from the **Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)** depending on your factory and its environmental impact.

The requirements depend on:

*   Type of industry
    
*   Production capacity
    
*   Fuel
    
*   Emissions
    
*   Wastewater
    
*   Solid waste
    
*   Hazardous materials
    
*   Dust
    
*   Noise
    
*   Location
    

The KP One-Window portal specifically provides access to the Environmental Protection Agency-related approvals.

### For example

A small precast/block plant may have substantially different environmental requirements from:

*   Asphalt plant
    
*   Cement-related manufacturing
    
*   Steel melting/re-rolling

9\. Factory registration with Labour Department
===============================================

Once the factory is established, factory registration is required under the applicable KP factory legislation.

The **KP Directorate of Labour** provides a dedicated factory-registration service.

The government's published service information lists these documents for factory registration:

1.  Form A — Notice of Occupation
    
2.  Form K — Certificate of Stability
    
3.  List of workers
    
4.  List of machinery
    
5.  Site plan
    
6.  Request letter to Chief Inspector of Factories
    

The published service standard states **10 days** for a complete factory-registration application.

12\. KPRA registration — if you provide taxable services
========================================================

Don't confuse **manufacturing sales** with **services**.

If your company also provides taxable services in KP—for example certain construction/contracting services—KPRA requirements can become relevant.

KPRA administers KP sales tax on services.

For a company, KPRA's published registration documentation includes:

*   Company NTN
    
*   Directors' CNIC/NTN information
    
*   Electricity bill
    
*   Company letterhead
    
*   Bank statement/certificate
    
*   SECP incorporation certificate
    
*   Form 21/Form 29
    
*   Property ownership/lease documentation
