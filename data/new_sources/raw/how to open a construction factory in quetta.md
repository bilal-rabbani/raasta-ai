2\. Choose an appropriate industrial location
=============================================

For a manufacturing factory, an **industrial estate is normally preferable to ordinary residential/commercial land**.

Quetta has industrial activity around **Takhtani Industrial Estate / Quetta Industrial & Trading Estate**. Current business listings show construction-material manufacturing already operating there, including cement, PVC/HDPE pipe and other industrial units.

For example:

QSE Cement Factory is listed in Takhtani Industrial Estate, while Shaheen HDPE Poly Roll pipe Factory and Bolan PVC pipe Factory are also located there.

The **Directorate of Small Industries Balochistan** is also located in Quetta's industrial area and can be a useful government contact point:

The Balochistan Industries & Commerce Department specifically states that it assists investors with establishing industries, industrial estates, permits and regulatory requirements.

3\. Obtain land / industrial plot
=================================

You need proper evidence of your right to use the premises:

*   Allotment/lease documents
    
*   Sale deed/registry, where applicable
    
*   Possession documents
    
*   Site plan
    
*   CNIC/company documents
    
*   Industrial-estate NOC, where applicable
    
*   Land-use confirmation
    
*   Utility availability
    

### Important

Don't assume that owning a plot automatically gives you permission to operate a factory there.

For QDA-controlled property, the Quetta Development Authority's process includes obtaining an **Estate Directorate NOC** and then obtaining a site plan and building permit.

4\. Choose your business structure
==================================

You have several possibilities:

### Option A — Sole proprietorship

Suitable for a small owner-operated business.

### Option B — Partnership/AOP

Useful if two or more people are investing.

### Option C — Private Limited Company

Generally preferable for a medium/large manufacturing project because it separates the business from the owners and is easier to structure for multiple investors and financing.

SECP currently provides online incorporation through its eZfile system. A single-member private company is also possible.

### Basic company sequence

**Name → incorporation → incorporation certificate → bank account → FBR registration → provincial registrations → industrial permissions**

6\. Obtain industrial permission
================================

This is an important Quetta/Balochistan requirement.

The **Balochistan Industries (Control on Establishment and Enlargement) Ordinance, 1963** provides that an industrial undertaking covered by the law cannot be established or enlarged without prior written government permission.

Therefore, your project should be submitted through the relevant **Balochistan Industries & Commerce Department** process rather than simply starting construction.

The department lists:

*   Industrial licensing
    
*   Environmental/safety compliance support
    
*   Investor facilitation
    
*   Industrial-estate assistance
    

among its services.

7\. QDA / building approval
===========================

If the factory is within QDA jurisdiction, you need to deal with the **Quetta Development Authority Building Control Section**.

The QDA's published procedure for a building permit requires:

1.  Application
    
2.  Estate Directorate NOC
    
3.  QDA site plan
    
4.  Detailed architectural drawings
    
5.  Drawings signed/stamped by a PCATP-registered architect
    
6.  CNIC
    
7.  Scrutiny by Building Control
    
8.  Building/layout approval
    
9.  Applicable map/security/possession fees
    
10.  Building permit
    

For a factory, you should also have appropriately qualified engineers prepare the structural, electrical, mechanical and fire-safety aspects.

8\. Environmental approval
==========================

This is a **major requirement** for a manufacturing facility.

Contact the **Balochistan Environment Department / EPA** before construction/production.

The Environment Department states that it handles environmental impact assessment and environmental permits/compliance for industries and businesses.

Depending on your factory and its scale/process, the environmental assessment may involve:

*   Environmental screening
    
*   IEE
    
*   EIA
    
*   Environmental approval/clearance
    
*   Pollution-control requirements
    
*   Waste-management arrangements
    
*   Dust/emission controls
    
*   Wastewater arrangements
    
*   Noise controls
    

### Particularly important for construction-material factories

If you are producing:

*   Cement products
    
*   Concrete
    
*   Blocks
    
*   Asphalt
    
*   Tiles
    
*   Stone/crushing products
    
*   Chemicals
    
*   Paint/coatings
    
*   Pipes/plastics
    

the environmental requirements can differ substantially.

**Do the environmental classification before ordering machinery.**

9\. Factory/labour registration
===============================

Once you have a manufacturing operation with workers, the **Balochistan Factories Act, 2021** becomes important.

The Act regulates labour in factories throughout Balochistan.

The Labour & Manpower Department states that it is responsible for labour-law enforcement and workplace safety/health.

You should therefore prepare for:

*   Factory registration
    
*   Factory inspection
    
*   Appointment/details of factory management
    
*   Worker records
    
*   Working-hours compliance
    
*   Safety arrangements
    
*   Machine guarding
    
*   PPE
    
*   First aid
    
*   Emergency exits
    
*   Fire safety
    
*   Sanitation
    
*   Drinking water
    
*   Accident reporting
    
*   Worker welfare requirements
    

The factory should not simply start hiring and operating without completing the applicable labour registrations.

10\. EOBI
=========

If your establishment falls within EOBI's coverage, register the employer and eligible employees with the **Employees' Old-Age Benefits Institution (EOBI)**.

EOBI provides an online employer-registration facility.

11\. Balochistan social security
================================

You should also check registration with **Balochistan Employees' Social Security Institution (BESSI)** based on your workforce and establishment.

The Balochistan Labour Department identifies BESSI as one of its attached departments and describes worker social-security/health-insurance functions.

12\. FBR — NTN / income tax
===========================

Register the business with FBR.

For a company/AOP, FBR requires information such as:

*   Business name
    
*   Business address
    
*   Principal activity
    
*   Industrial establishment address
    
*   Incorporation/registration documents
    
*   Directors/partners information
    
*   Bank information
    

For a company, the normal sequence is:

**SECP incorporation → business bank account → FBR registration/NTN**

13\. Sales tax registration
===========================

For a manufacturing business, carefully determine whether you are required to register for sales tax.

FBR's current Sales Tax registration process requires business information and, for manufacturers, can include:

*   Bank account certificate
    
*   Business details
    
*   GPS-tagged premises photographs
    
*   Electricity/gas consumer information
    
*   GPS-tagged machinery photographs
    
*   Industrial electricity/gas meter information
    

After registration, biometric verification through a NADRA e-Sahulat centre is required within the specified period.

**Do not rely on old online articles for the sales-tax threshold or rate** because these rules can change.

14\. Balochistan Revenue Authority
==================================

BRA primarily deals with **sales tax on services**, rather than replacing FBR's federal sales-tax system for manufactured goods.

BRA states that it administers Balochistan sales tax on services.

If your factory also provides taxable services—for example certain engineering, contracting, installation or other services—you should determine whether BRA registration applies.

15\. Electricity — QESCO
========================

A factory will normally require an industrial electricity connection.

Plan for:

*   Load calculation
    
*   Industrial connection
    
*   Transformer
    
*   HT/LT equipment as applicable
    
*   Distribution panels
    
*   Earthing
    
*   Protection systems
    
*   Power factor correction
    
*   Generator/backup

Before selecting machinery, calculate:

**Total connected load + starting current + future expansion requirement.**

Don't size the electrical system only from the running power of the machines.

16\. Gas, if required
=====================

If your process requires natural gas, investigate industrial gas availability with the relevant gas utility before finalizing the site.

Do **not** assume that gas is available simply because a residential/commercial connection exists nearby.

20\. Boiler / pressure equipment
================================

If you use a boiler or other covered pressure equipment, additional approval/registration may apply.

The **Balochistan Boilers and Pressure Vessels Act, 2015** requires covered boilers to be registered and certified before use.

So:

**Boiler/pressure vessel → inspection → registration/certificate → qualified operation**

Do not install a boiler and start using it without checking the applicable requirements.
