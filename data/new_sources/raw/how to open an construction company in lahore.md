    # Starting a Construction-Material Factory in Lahore

## 1. Decide exactly what the factory will manufacture

Before buying land or machinery, define:

- Product
- Production capacity/day
- Raw materials
- Manufacturing process
- Machinery
- Number of employees
- Electricity requirement
- Water requirement
- Fuel requirement
- Waste generated
- Noise/dust/emissions
- Storage requirements
- Expected investment
- Target customers

For example:

| Factory type | Typical products |
|---|---|
| Concrete products | Blocks, pavers, kerbs |
| Precast factory | Beams, slabs, columns, drains |
| Steel fabrication | Gates, structures, trusses |
| Pipe factory | PVC/concrete pipes |
| Tile factory | Floor/wall tiles |
| Cement products | Blocks, rings, decorative products |
| Aluminium/UPVC | Doors/windows |
| Construction chemicals | Adhesives, waterproofing |
| Asphalt-related | Asphalt/construction materials |

This decision should come first, because environmental, fire, machinery, utility and standards requirements can differ substantially.

## 2. Choose an appropriate industrial location

Do not simply purchase agricultural/residential land and start construction.

The property needs to be suitable for industrial use and your particular manufacturing activity.

For Lahore, industrial areas can include places such as:

- Sundar Industrial Estate
- Quaid-e-Azam Industrial Estate / Kot Lakhpat
- Other approved industrial estates/zones
- Appropriate industrial areas outside the main urban core

For example, PIEDMC operates industrial-estate infrastructure in Lahore, including Sundar Industrial Estate. PIEDMC

### Why industrial zoning is important

You need to check:

- Land-use/zoning status
- Permitted industrial activity
- Plot size
- Building restrictions
- Road access
- Heavy vehicle access
- Electricity availability
- Gas availability, if required
- Water
- Sewerage/drainage
- Fire access
- Environmental restrictions
- Distance from residential areas
- Future expansion

For Lahore, LDA regulations and applicable building/zoning requirements need to be checked before construction. LDA currently publishes its building and zoning regulations.

## 3. Decide your legal business structure

You can operate through, depending on your circumstances:

### Option A — Sole proprietorship

Simplest structure for a small business.

### Option B — Partnership/AOP

For multiple owners.

### Option C — Private Limited Company

Usually preferable for a substantial manufacturing operation because it provides a separate legal entity and is easier to structure for investors/partners.

### Option D — Single Member Company

Possible where there is one owner.

SECP currently provides online company incorporation through its eZfile system, including name reservation and incorporation.

## 5. Obtain FBR NTN

After incorporation, the company needs its tax registration.

SECP's system is integrated with FBR for NTN issuance when the incorporation information is complete and accurate.

For tax registration, FBR identifies information/documents including:

- Incorporation certificate
- Directors' CNICs
- Company bank account information
- Business premises ownership/tenancy evidence
- Utility bill
- Company email
- Business activity
- Industrial establishment address

## 7. Sales Tax registration

This is particularly important for a manufacturing business.

Whether and when registration is required depends on your taxable supplies and applicable exemptions/rules.

FBR states that persons in the relevant taxable sectors making taxable supplies are subject to Sales Tax registration, subject to applicable exemptions.

For a serious manufacturing operation, have a tax practitioner determine:

- STRN requirement
- Sales-tax treatment of your products
- Input tax
- Output tax
- Invoices
- Returns
- Exemptions
- Withholding obligations

Don't rely on an old percentage or threshold from an internet article because tax rules can change.

## 11. Environmental approval — VERY IMPORTANT

This is one of the most important parts of establishing a factory.

You should determine the environmental category before construction/operation.

Depending on the project, Punjab Environmental Protection Department may require:

- Environmental approval
- IEE
- EIA
- Environmental management measures
- Emission controls
- Waste-management arrangements
- Wastewater controls
- Monitoring/compliance

Punjab EPA's current process describes IEE/EIA preparation, submission, scrutiny, review and environmental approval.

The EPD is responsible for reviewing IEE/EIA reports and issuing environmental approvals.

### Why you shouldn't guess the category

For example, Punjab EPD's current records include approvals/assessment work for:

- Manufacturing units
- Pipe manufacturing
- Steel-related manufacturing
- Industrial chemicals
- Industrial warehouses
- Other Lahore industrial projects

So your exact product/process matters.

## 33. If you also want to undertake construction contracts

This is different from merely manufacturing construction materials.

If your company will actually execute engineering/construction works for clients, you may need Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC) constructor/operator registration, depending on the nature of work.

PEC currently provides categories including C-3 through C-6 for applicable constructor firms.

PEC's current registration portal lists requirements such as:

- Fee voucher
- Financial records
- Affidavit
- Machinery/equipment list
- Organizational chart
- NTN
- And, for higher categories, SECP registration, financial soundness and project/experience information.

So:

> **Factory ≠ construction contractor.**

You only need the contractor-side registration if you are going to undertake the relevant engineering/construction work.
