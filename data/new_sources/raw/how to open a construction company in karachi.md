2\. Complete the business structure
===================================

You normally start by establishing the legal business.

For a serious factory, a **Private Limited Company** is often the appropriate structure, although an individual proprietorship or partnership can also be possible depending on the circumstances.

The company-registration process is handled by Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP).

SECP's current incorporation process is digital and includes:

1.  Create SECP account
    
2.  Reserve company name
    
3.  Prepare incorporation information
    
4.  Prepare Memorandum and Articles
    
5.  Enter directors/shareholders
    
6.  Submit incorporation application
    
7.  Pay government fee
    
8.  Digital signing/submission
    
9.  Receive Certificate of Incorporation
    

SECP describes the incorporation process as a four-step digital process.

[SECP Company Registration](https://www.secp.gov.pk/start-your-company/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

3\. Obtain FBR tax registration
===============================

You will need the appropriate Federal Board of Revenue registrations.

For a company, the **NTN** is integrated with the SECP incorporation process. SECP states that company information is forwarded to FBR and NTN is issued when the information is complete and accurate.

Depending on your business, you may also need:

*   Sales Tax registration / STRN
    
*   Income-tax registration
    
*   Withholding-tax compliance
    
*   Sales-tax invoicing/returns
    
*   Tax accounting records
    

The exact sales-tax position depends on the products and transactions, so this should be confirmed with an FBR tax professional before starting production.

7\. Environmental approval — extremely important
================================================

This is one of the most important parts of establishing a factory in Sindh.

The **Sindh Environmental Protection Agency (SEPA)** regulates environmental approvals.

SEPA explains that projects can fall into different categories:

### Environmental screening

Some relatively lower-impact projects may require environmental screening/checklist.

### IEE

Some manufacturing projects require an **Initial Environmental Examination (IEE)**.

### EIA

Higher-impact projects may require an **Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)**.

For example, SEPA's current guidance identifies cement plants, chemical projects, fertilizer plants and certain large manufacturing projects among EIA categories, while various smaller manufacturing activities fall under IEE or screening depending on the project.

SEPA also states that construction/development activity in Sindh requires the relevant environmental approval/NOC before commencement, depending on the project.

So **don't assume every construction-material factory has the same environmental approval**.

Once you have a manufacturing operation and workers, labour/factory legislation applies.

The **Sindh Factories Act, 2015** governs factories in Sindh and gives factory inspectors powers concerning factory premises, machinery and prescribed records.

You should therefore plan for:

*   Factory registration/notification as applicable
    
*   Occupier/manager designation
    
*   Worker records
    
*   Working-hour compliance
    
*   Safety arrangements
    
*   Machine guarding
    
*   Emergency exits
    
*   First aid
    
*   Workplace cleanliness
    
*   Accident records
    
*   Safety signage
    
*   Required registers/records
    
*   Labour inspections
    

Do this **before production**, not after an inspection.

9\. SESSI registration
======================

If your workers fall within the applicable social-security coverage, register with **Sindh Employees' Social Security Institution (SESSI)**.

SESSI describes its coverage as extending across industries and commercial units in Sindh.

The SESSI regulations also contain an employer-registration form requiring information such as:

*   Firm name
    
*   Employer
    
*   Business address
    
*   Nature of business
    
*   Number of employees
    
*   Employer signature/stamp
